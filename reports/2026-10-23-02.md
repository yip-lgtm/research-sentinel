## 標題 / Title：效力 36% 為何換來 13% 兒童死亡率下降：瘧疾疫苗的真實世界機制

### 主題 / Topic
RTS,S 對 5 至 17 個月兒童的臨床瘧疾效力僅 36.3%，加納、肯亞、馬拉威推行四年後全因兒童死亡率卻降 13%。保護力究竟落在哪條死亡路徑？效價衰減如何侵蝕它？

RTS,S showed 36.3% efficacy against clinical malaria in young children, yet four years of rollout in Ghana, Kenya and Malawi cut all-cause child mortality by 13%. Where does the protection land on the pathway to death, and how does waning erode it?

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
【機制】保護集中在重症，且隨時間衰減。三期加強劑組臨床效力 36.3%（95% CI 31.8–40.5）、重症 32.2%（13.7–46.9）；無加強劑組跌至 28.3%、重症僅 1.1%（信賴區間跨越零）。死亡改善全靠重症一端，加強劑是唯一拉回重症保護的因素。

Efficacy concentrates on severe disease and decays. The boosted arm reached 36.3% (95% CI 31.8-40.5) clinically and 32.2% for severe malaria; the unboosted arm fell to 28.3% and 1.1%, an interval spanning zero. The mortality gain travels through the severe-malaria channel.

【機制】13% 遠小於效力降幅，因五歲以下死亡不全屬瘧疾。首兩年重症入院減 32%（5–51%），同期全因死亡只減 9%（0–18%）；四年後全因死亡減 13%，約每八宗兒童死亡避免一宗。效力數病例數，死亡被分母中其他死因稀釋。

The mortality drop is far smaller because not every under-five death is malaria. Severe-malaria admissions fell 32% (5-51%) in the first two pilot years while all-cause mortality fell only 9% (0-18%). Efficacy counts episodes, not deaths.

【機制】抗原多樣性稀釋 RTS,S。NEJM 三期配對等位效力 50.3%（34.6–62.3），不配對僅 33.4%（29.3–37.2），當地配對等位低於 10%。R21 改打保守 NANP 表位並加乙肝表面抗原載體，12 個月效力升至 75%（季節疫點）與 68%（標準疫點）。

Antigenic diversity dilutes RTS,S. NEJM phase 3 showed 50.3% efficacy against allele-matched parasites but 33.4% against mismatched ones; under 10% of local parasites matched. R21 targets the conserved NANP repeat with an HBsAg carrier, lifting 12-month efficacy to 75% and 68%.

【替代解釋】現場 13% 可能混入非疫苗因素。喀麥隆全國差分研究粗略顯示重症瘧疾減 15%、瘧疾死亡減 26%，調整後不顯著（重症 −0.54，95% CI −11.07 至 9.99，p=0.920），作者歸因選擇偏誤；化學預防擴張與蚊帳覆蓋變化皆可貢獻。三國試點雖為叢集隨機，死亡監測並非盲法。

The field 13% may absorb non-vaccine effects. A Cameroon difference-in-differences study showed 15% fewer severe malaria cases crudely, but adjusted estimates were null (severe malaria -0.54, 95% CI -11.07 to 9.99, p=0.920), attributed to selection bias. Mortality surveillance was unblinded.

【敏感度】42 國模型三情境：效力恆定 40% 四年後歸零，每千名兒童避免 0.6 宗死亡（0.5–0.9）；由 80% 起每年跌 20 個百分點避免 0.9 宗（0.6–1.2）；恆定 40% 十年避免 1.1 宗（0.8–1.5）。結論在保護持續時間內成立：若四年後歸零，高初始效力被抹平。

Modelling across 42 countries: efficacy constant at 40% for four years then zero averts 0.6 deaths per 1000 children; 80% falling 20 points a year averts 0.9; constant 40% for ten years averts 1.1. The conclusion holds while protection outlasts waning.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
規模與案例：WHO 指 25 個非洲流行國家已把瘧疾疫苗納入兒童免疫程式，每年目標逾 1000 萬名兒童；2024 年估計 438,000 名非洲兒童死於此病。三國試點以 158 個叢集運作（加納 66 區、肯亞 46 個次級行政區、馬拉威 46 個門診群），652,673 名兒童至少接種一劑、494,745 名接種三劑，死亡監測回報 13,198 宗死亡；第一劑覆蓋 76%、79%、73%。

Scale and case: WHO reports 25 endemic African countries now offer malaria vaccines, targeting over 10 million children a year; an estimated 438,000 African children died of malaria in 2024. The pilot ran 158 clusters, reaching 652,673 children with one dose and 494,745 with three, capturing 13,198 deaths; first-dose coverage 76%, 79%, 73%.

肯亞分母陷阱：36 個月內接種 818,648 劑。以服務人次為分母，第 1 至 4 劑覆蓋率 96%、87%、78%、39%；改以人口為分母即跌至 78%、68%、57%、24%，近四分之一社區兒童未接種。

Kenya, a denominator trap: 818,648 doses over 36 months. Against service users, coverage for doses 1-4 was 96%, 87%, 78%, 39%; against eligible population it fell to 78%, 68%, 57%, 24% — close to a quarter of community children unvaccinated.

成本與價格：每劑 5 美元、疫苗由捐助資助計，每名完整接種兒童增量財務成本 11.50 美元（加納）至 13.69 美元（馬拉維）；非疫苗交付成本每劑 0.23 至 0.71 美元，經濟成本為財務成本三至五倍；引入成本佔總財務成本 33% 至 71%。

Cost and price: at USD 5 per dose, donor-funded, incremental financial cost per fully vaccinated child ran from USD 11.50 (Ghana) to USD 13.69 (Malawi); non-vaccine delivery cost USD 0.23-0.71 per dose, economic costs three to five times higher; introduction costs 33% to 71% of total financial costs.

【推論】R21 每劑降至 2.99 美元後，四劑全程每名兒童疫苗購置支出可再減約 8 美元。Gavi 稱議價省最多 9,000 萬美元、可多買 3,000 萬劑，五年內多保護近 700 萬名兒童。另一邊，肯亞第 4 劑人口分母覆蓋僅 24%，補至第 3 劑的 57% 等於把已驗證的重症保護接回。

Inference: with R21 at USD 2.99 per dose, purchase outlay per child falls by roughly USD 8 across four doses. Gavi puts the negotiation at up to USD 90 million saved and 30 million extra doses, protecting nearly 7 million more children within five years. Kenya's fourth dose reached 24% of eligible children; lifting it to 57% reconnects a demonstrated mechanism.

### 簡評 / Commentary
可信度：三國叢集隨機試點四年評估證據最強，但未取得全文，只以 WHO 摘要與期刊索引交叉引述；13% 與每八宗避免一宗均屬該評估結論。爭議點：36.3% 是三期 48 個月中位追蹤的臨床效力，32% 是現場兩年的重症入院降幅，口徑不同，不可並列為「效力」。缺口：未取得 2026 年 Lancet 完整方法與信賴區間，亦無加強劑覆蓋率與死亡下降的劑量反應分析。

Confidence: the four-year cluster-randomised pilot evaluation is strongest, but the full text was unreachable, so the 13% figure rests on the WHO summary cross-checked against the journal index. Contested point: 36.3% is clinical efficacy over a median 48-month follow-up, while 32% is a two-year reduction in severe-malaria admissions in the field; these are different quantities and must not be listed side by side as efficacy. Gaps: no complete methods or confidence intervals from the 2026 Lancet paper, and no dose-response analysis of booster coverage.
