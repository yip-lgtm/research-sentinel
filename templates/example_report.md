## 標題：同樣叫 MPhil，研究型與授課型通往博士的規則並不相同
# Title: Not All MPhil Degrees Lead to a PhD — Course-Level Rules Differ Across Cambridge Engineering
（2026-10-22 15:00 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic
本報告考察一個在碩博士銜接上常被想當然的事實：攻讀 Cambridge 工程類 MPhil 後，能否直接銜接博士，
以及名稱是否意味著研究型。焦點是官方課程目錄的分類標籤與各課程的 continuing 條款之間是否一致，
以及這種差異對產學合作、博士招生篩選與工程人才供給的實際影響。
This report examines an assumption often taken for granted in master's-to-doctoral progression: whether an
MPhil in engineering at Cambridge leads to PhD entry, and whether the degree name implies a research route.
It checks whether the official course-directory labels agree with each course's continuing-registration rules,
and why that gap matters for industry collaboration, PhD shortlisting, and the supply of engineers.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- **標籤不一致**：Cambridge 官方課程目錄同時提供「Taught（188 門）」與「Research（169 門）」兩個篩選維度，
  另有獨立的 Course Level 維度（Master's 221、Doctoral 117、Diploma 11、Certificate/Award 10）。
  Taught 與 Research 是平行的兩類，不是研究型的上層分類。
  Labels disagree: the official directory offers "Taught" and "Research" as parallel filters, plus a separate
  course-level dimension (Master's, Doctoral, Diploma, Certificate). Neither implies the other.
- **標為 Research 也不保證能銜接博士**：Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology MPhil（標為 Research）明文
  規定「does not "convert" to a PhD」，學生須重新申請。
  Being labelled Research does not guarantee progression: the Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology MPhil
  explicitly states it "does not convert" to a PhD and must be reapplied for.
- **標為 Taught 也不排除研究成分**：Advanced Chemical Engineering MPhil（標為 Taught）仍要求學生於
  3 月至 8 月進行全職研究項目並提交論文，但同時明文規定該學期「cannot be counted as one year of a PhD
  research degree」。
  Being labelled Taught does not exclude research: the Advanced Chemical Engineering MPhil requires a
  full-time research project and dissertation, yet states it "cannot be counted as one year of a PhD".
- **學術門檻不同**：Electrical and Electronic Engineering MPhil（Taught）要求整體成績達 70 percent
  方符合博士銜接期望；Engineering MPhil（Research）則只要求「pass the MPhil programme」。
  Different academic gates: the EEE MPhil expects an overall mark of 70 percent for PhD entry, whereas the
  Engineering MPhil expects only that students "pass the MPhil programme".

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
【推論】若企業與合作院校以「MPhil = 博士預備」作通則篩選人才，會在研究型課程上誤收不具銜接資格者，
同時在授課型課程上誤拒具備研究產出能力的學生；錯誤成本體現在額外招生與培訓支出、以及合作項目啟動延誤。
【推論】對以博士為上游的工程研究管道而言，MPhil 銜接規則的逐課程差異意味着一條更長、
成本更高的博士培養路徑，直接影響公共研究資助的產出效率。
【推論】對香港及內地院校設計類似「碩博連讀」通道時，本案例提供一個具體對照：課程分類、銜接資格與
研究產出三者必須分別訂明，不能靠名稱推斷。
【推論】If employers and partner universities screen candidates on the general rule "MPhil means PhD-track",
they will wrongly admit students who hold a research-labelled course that cannot convert, while wrongly
rejecting students from taught courses who can produce thesis-grade research. The cost appears as
unnecessary recruitment and training spend, and as delay in starting joint projects.
【推論】For any engineering research pipeline fed by doctoral study, course-level variation in MPhil
continuing rules means a longer and more expensive route to the doctorate, directly affecting how efficiently
public research funding is converted into output.
【推論】For institutions in Hong Kong and mainland China designing integrated master's-to-doctoral tracks,
this case offers a concrete reference point: classification, progression eligibility, and research output
must each be specified separately, and none may be inferred from the degree name alone.

### 簡評 / Commentary
可信度評估：中高。條款均取自 Cambridge 官方課程目錄的原文，可逐字覆核；但本次未取得每門課程的
入學年份、學費與實際錄取數據，亦未能核對其他院校的同類條款，因此只覆蓋單一院校、單一學科。
爭議點：課程目錄的 Taught/Research 分類並非學術認可的統一定義，屬校內行政標籤，
不同院校同名學位規則可能完全不同，不應外推。
資料缺口：PolyU、CUHK 的對應課程頁在本次抓取中受阻；Imperial、Stanford 的年限定義未取得。

本次發現已寫回知識庫章節 04（graduate tier）；知識庫其餘進度不屬本報告範圍。
Confidence: moderate-to-high. All quoted rules come from the official Cambridge course directory and can be
checked verbatim. This report covers a single institution and a single faculty; no entry-year, tuition, or
actual-admission data was obtained, and comparable clauses at other institutions were not retrieved.
Contested point: the directory's Taught/Research labels are internal administrative categories, not a
standardised academic definition. Courses with the same name elsewhere may follow entirely different rules,
so no generalisation should be made.
Data gaps: the corresponding course pages at PolyU and CUHK were blocked during this crawl; programme
length definitions at Imperial and Stanford were not retrieved.
Next steps: build a cross-regime table of comparable continuing clauses for Hong Kong, the UK, and the US,
then test whether the name-misleading hypothesis holds at other institutions.
