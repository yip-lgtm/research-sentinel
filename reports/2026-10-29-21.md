### 主題 / Topic
中國飲用水嘅高氟係地球化學結果，唔係工業污染。螢石溶出嘅氟堵唔到，咁改水降氟推咗幾十年，點解 8–12 歲兒童氟斑牙仲有近三成？

High-fluoride drinking water in China is geogenic, not industrial pollution. If fluorite-derived fluoride cannot be engineered away, why does dental fluorosis still affect nearly 30% of children after decades of water-improvement programmes?

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- 【機制】10,288 個地下水樣本（354 篇文獻）平均 F⁻ 1.17 mg/L，47.24% 超中國 1 mg/L 標準；螢石溶解貢獻 62.08%–86.18%，蒸發最高 4.42%、陽離子交換最高 13.07%。
- 【機制】四類水文地化組別氟水平相差近六倍（0.56、0.89、3.05、3.55 mg/L）：高氟熱點同慢流速、高蒸發、陽離子交換綁定，唔係均勻分佈。
- 【機制】劑量-反應已量化：74 篇研究、20,932 名兒童合併 SMD −0.45；個體尿氟每升多 1 mg，IQ 少 1.63 分（低偏倚子集 1.14 分）。
- 【替代解釋】改水失效唔係「氟減唔到」，而係設施運作唔到：29 省 136 縣 27,817 名兒童中，正常運作達標村氟斑牙 22.28%，運作不良或超標村 38.74%，未改水村 40.03%。
- 【機制】滯後效應：改水令水氟由 2.34 降至 0.67 mg/L、氟斑牙由 56.7% 降至 28.8%；要 11–20 年才到 21.5%（OR 0.15），逾 20 年先到 10.4%（OR 0.06）。
- 【敏感度】1.5 mg/L 以下有冇效應視乎信邊批數據：全體樣本中飲用水 <1.5 mg/L 為 null，但只揀低偏倚研究時仍為負相關。取 null 則達標區冇神經認知收益；取負相關則中國 1.0 mg/L 與 WHO 1.5 mg/L 之間存在真實健康差距。我無法判定邊個啱。

- A 10,288-sample compilation (354 papers) found mean F- of 1.17 mg/L, 47.24% above China's 1 mg/L standard; fluorite dissolution supplied 62-86% of enrichment, evaporation 4.42% and cation exchange 13.07% at most.
- Fluoride varied almost sixfold across four hydrogeochemical groups (0.56-3.55 mg/L); hotspots track slow flow, evaporation and cation exchange rather than uniform geology.
- The dose-response is quantified: 74 studies and 20,932 children pool to SMD -0.45, and each 1 mg/L higher urinary fluoride means 1.63 fewer IQ points (1.14 in low-risk-of-bias studies).
- Failure is not inability to remove fluoride but plant failure to run: fluorosis was 22.28% in well-operated compliant villages, 38.74% in malfunctioning or over-limit villages and 40.03% where no scheme existed.
- Effects lag: water improvement cut water fluoride from 2.34 to 0.67 mg/L and fluorosis from 56.7% to 28.8%, but 21.5% (OR 0.15) took 11-20 years and 10.4% (OR 0.06) over 20 years.
- Whether effects persist below 1.5 mg/L turns on which evidence is trusted: null across all studies, still inverse in low-risk-of-bias subsets. The null removes neurocognitive benefit in compliant areas; the inverse makes the 1.0 vs 1.5 mg/L gap a real difference. I cannot adjudicate this.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
- 【推論】以全國 47,057 個減氟供水方案（覆蓋 79,190 個村）計，若其中約三分之一重演上述失效比例，近 26,000 個村嘅水氟控制形同虛設。此為算術外推，假設監測比例可推廣至全國。
- 【推論】案例：山西山陰縣 8.3 ± 1.9 mg/L 高氟村 253 名兒童平均 IQ 101 ± 16，對照村（0.5 mg/L）196 人為 105 ± 15，差 4 分（p<0.05）；同縣高砷村 95 ± 17 更低，顯示共暴露會遮蔽氟效應。
- 【推論】政策評估窗口會扭曲成效：以當前 8–12 歲兒童衡量，會錯判只運行十年嘅方案失敗（61.5%→21.5% 仍未到二十年嘅 10.4%）。
- 【推論】機制上原地減氟或更省：膠萊盆地提出挖 ditch 縮短淺層地下水徑流、降低水位以削弱蒸發富集；但只有土壤淋溶實驗（718 水樣、46 採樣期、誤差 <5%），未見改造前後現場實測氟值，屬機制合理、實證不足。
- 本題未找到可引用的減氟廠站造價、單位供水成本或現存病村數目，故不作估算。

- With 47,057 schemes covering 79,190 villages, if about a third failed as above, roughly 26,000 villages would have ineffective fluoride control; this extrapolates the surveyed rate nationwide.
- Case: in Shanyin county, Shanxi, 253 children at 8.3 +/- 1.9 mg/L averaged 101 +/- 16 IQ against 105 +/- 15 in the 0.5 mg/L control, a 4-point gap; the high-arsenic village scored 95 +/- 17, showing co-exposure masking.
- Evaluation windows distort results: measuring current 8-12 year-olds would wrongly fail ten-year-old schemes, which have not reached the 10.4% seen after twenty years.
- In-situ treatment may be cheaper: the Jiaolai Basin proposes ditches to shorten shallow-groundwater runoff and lower levels, but rests on leaching experiments (718 samples, 46 periods, under 5% error) with no monitored before-and-after field fluoride values.
- No verifiable defluoridation plant cost, unit water cost or count of remaining endemic villages was found, so none is estimated.

### 簡評 / Commentary
可信度：劑量-反應同改水成效有系統評價同全國監測支撐，較穩；成因量化（螢石 62–86%）係統計編譯，各研究方法不一；IQ 多屬橫斷面，不能推因果。缺口：減氟廠站成本、剩餘病村數目、中國 ≥1.0 mg/L 暴露人口官方最新數字均未取得；膠萊 ditch 改造現場數據未公開。呢個題目按配額規則落在化學／化工域，重心係水-岩反應機制，唔係法規現況。

Credibility: the dose-response and water-improvement effects rest on systematic reviews and national surveillance; the 62-86% attribution is a statistical compilation of heterogeneous studies, and the IQ data are largely cross-sectional. Gaps: plant costs, remaining endemic villages and current official counts above 1.0 mg/L were not obtained, and the Jiaolai ditch field data are unpublished.
