## 標題：效力 36% 為何換來 13% 兒童死亡率下降：瘧疾疫苗的真實世界機制
# Title: Why 36% Efficacy Yielded a 13% Drop in Child Mortality

（2026-10-23 02:30 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic
RTS,S 在第三期試驗對 5 至 17 個月兒童的臨床瘧疾效力只有 36.3%，為何加納、肯亞、馬拉威三國試點推行四年後卻見到 13% 全因兒童死亡率下降？本報告追問保護力落在哪條死亡路徑、抗體效價衰減如何侵蝕它，以及疫苗價格的邊際作用。

RTS,S showed 36.3% efficacy against clinical malaria in children aged 5-17 months in phase 3, yet four years of the Ghana, Kenya and Malawi pilot delivered a 13% drop in all-cause child mortality. Where does that protection land on the pathway to death, how does waning titre erode it, and what does price change?

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
【機制】效力集中在重症，且隨時間衰減。第三期有加強劑組對臨床瘧疾效力 36.3%（95% CI 31.8–40.5），對重症瘧疾 32.2%（13.7–46.9）；無加強劑組臨床效力跌至 28.3%（23.3–32.9），重症效力只剩 1.1%（−23.0 至 20.5，跨越零）。死亡下降靠重症那一端，而加強劑是唯一把重症保護拉回來的因素。

Efficacy concentrates on severe disease and decays over time. In phase 3 the boosted arm reached 36.3% efficacy (95% CI 31.8-40.5) against clinical malaria and 32.2% (13.7-46.9) against severe malaria; the unboosted arm fell to 28.3% (23.3-32.9) clinically and just 1.1% (-23.0 to 20.5) for severe disease, a confidence interval spanning zero. Mortality gains travel through the severe-malaria channel, and the booster is the only intervention that restores it.

【機制】死亡率降幅遠小於效力降幅，因為五歲以下死亡並不全屬瘧疾。試點首兩年重症瘧疾入院減 32%（5–51%），同期全因死亡只減 9%（0–18%）；四年後全因死亡減 13%，約每八宗兒童死亡避免一宗。臨床效力衡量的是病例數，死亡效力受分母中其他死因稀釋。

The mortality drop is far smaller than the efficacy drop because not every under-five death is malaria. In the first two pilot years severe-malaria admissions fell 32% (5-51%) while all-cause mortality fell only 9% (0-18%); at four years all-cause mortality was down 13%, roughly one child death in eight averted. Clinical efficacy counts episodes; mortality is diluted by the other causes of death in the denominator.

【機制】抗原多樣性稀釋了 RTS,S。NEJM 第三期顯示配對等位瘧疾效力 50.3%（34.6–62.3），不配對只有 33.4%（29.3–37.2），而當地配對等位低於 10%。R21 改以保守 NANP 表位為靶並加乙肝表面抗原載體，12 個月效力升至 75%（71–79，季節性疫點）與 68%（61–74，標準疫點），5 至 17 個月組最高（79% 與 75%），且抗體效價與效力相關。

Antigenic diversity dilutes RTS,S. NEJM phase 3 showed 50.3% efficacy (34.6-62.3) against allele-matched parasites but only 33.4% (29.3-37.2) against mismatched ones, and fewer than 10% of local parasites carried matched alleles. R21 instead targets the conserved NANP repeat with an HBsAg carrier, lifting 12-month efficacy to 75% (71-79, seasonal sites) and 68% (61-74, standard sites), peaking at 79% and 75% in the 5-17 month band, with antibody titre correlating with protection.

【替代解釋】現場的 13% 可能混入非疫苗因素。喀麥隆全國差分研究中粗略差分顯示重症瘧疾減 15%、瘧疾死亡減 26%，但調整後估計不顯著（重症 −0.54，95% CI −11.07 至 9.99，p=0.920），研究者歸因選擇偏誤與檢驗力不足；季節性化學預防擴張與蚊帳覆蓋變化都可貢獻。三國試點為叢集隨機設計，可信度明顯較高，但死亡監測並非盲法。

The field 13% may absorb non-vaccine effects. In a Cameroon nationwide difference-in-differences study crude estimates showed 15% fewer severe malaria cases and 26% fewer malaria deaths, but adjusted estimates were null (severe malaria -0.54, 95% CI -11.07 to 9.99, p=0.920), attributed by the authors to selection bias and low power; expanded seasonal chemoprevention and shifting net coverage can both contribute. The three-country pilot used cluster randomisation and is clearly stronger, but mortality surveillance was not blinded.

【敏感度】42 國模型（1 歲起每年接種，2021–2030）給出三個效力情境：效力恆定 40% 四年後歸零，每 1000 名兒童避免 0.6 宗死亡（0.5–0.9）、235.7 宗病例（187.8–305.9）；由 80% 起每年掉 20 個百分點，則為 0.9 宗死亡（0.6–1.2）、313.9 宗病例（249.8–406.6）；恆定 40% 十年，則為 1.1 宗死亡（0.8–1.5）、384.7 宗病例（311.7–496.5）。結論：維持效力時間比更高的初始效力更重要。假設範圍內結論成立——只要保護期長於衰減速度；若四年後歸零，高初始效力的優勢被抹平。

Modelling across 42 countries (annual vaccination from age one, 2021-2030) gives three efficacy scenarios: efficacy constant at 40% for four years then zero averts 0.6 deaths (0.5-0.9) and 235.7 cases (187.8-305.9) per 1000 children; efficacy starting at 80% and falling 20 points a year averts 0.9 deaths (0.6-1.2) and 313.9 cases (249.8-406.6); efficacy constant at 40% for ten years averts 1.1 deaths (0.8-1.5) and 384.7 cases (311.7-496.5). The conclusion holds while protection outlasts waning: if efficacy zeroes out after four years, a higher starting value buys nothing.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
規模：WHO 指出 25 個非洲流行國家已把瘧疾疫苗納入兒童免疫程式，每年目標逾 1000 萬名兒童；2024 年估計 438,000 名非洲兒童死於此病。截至 2024 年 12 月，17 個非洲國家已引入、另 8 個獲批支持，2023 至 2024 年運送 13,250,098 劑。

Scale: WHO reports 25 endemic African countries now offer malaria vaccines within childhood immunisation, targeting more than 10 million children a year; an estimated 438,000 African children died of malaria in 2024. By December 2024, 17 African countries had introduced the vaccine with eight more approved, and 13,250,098 doses shipped during 2023-2024.

三國試點案例：加納 66 區、肯亞 46 個次級行政區、馬拉維 46 個門診群共 158 個叢集，652,673 名兒童至少接種一劑、494,745 名接種三劑；死亡監測回報 13,198 宗死亡、26,285 宗住院。第一劑覆蓋 76%、79%、73%，第三劑 66%、62%、62%。

Pilot case: 158 clusters across 66 Ghanaian districts, 46 Kenyan sub-counties and 46 Malawian clinic catchments reached 652,673 children with at least one dose and 494,745 with three doses; mortality surveillance captured 13,198 deaths and 26,285 hospital admissions. First-dose coverage was 76%, 79% and 73%; third-dose 66%, 62% and 62%.

肯亞案例（分母陷阱）：36 個月內接種 818,648 劑。以服務人次為分母，第 1 至 4 劑覆蓋率 96%、87%、78%、39%；改以人口為分母即跌至 78%、68%、57%、24%，近四分之一社區兒童未接種。已接種 18 個月麻疹第 2 劑（覆蓋 59%）者中只有 66% 補上瘧疾第 3 劑。

Kenya case, a denominator trap: 818,648 doses were administered over 36 months. Measured against service users, coverage for doses 1 to 4 was 96%, 87%, 78% and 39%; against the eligible population it fell to 78%, 68%, 57% and 24%, leaving close to a quarter of community children unvaccinated. Among children who received the second measles dose at 18 months (59% coverage), only 66% completed malaria dose 3.

成本與價格：以每劑 5 美元、疫苗由捐助資助計，每名完整接種兒童增量財務成本為 11.50 美元（加納）至 13.69 美元（馬拉維）；非疫苗交付成本每劑 0.23 至 0.71 美元，經濟成本每劑為財務成本的三至五倍；引入成本佔總財務成本 33% 至 71%。

Cost and price: at USD 5 per dose with the vaccine donor-funded, the incremental financial cost per fully vaccinated child ran from USD 11.50 (Ghana) to USD 13.69 (Malawi); non-vaccine delivery cost USD 0.23-0.71 per dose, with economic costs three to five times financial costs; introduction costs were 33% to 71% of total financial costs.

【推論】R21 每劑降至 2.99 美元後，若交付結構不變，四劑全程每名兒童的疫苗購置支出可再減約 8 美元（4 劑乘 2.01 美元）。Gavi 稱議價省下最多 9,000 萬美元、可多買 3,000 萬劑，足以五年內多保護近 700 萬名兒童。

【推論】補齊加強劑的邊際回報高於擴大首劑覆蓋：肯亞第 4 劑人口分母覆蓋僅 24%，若補至第 3 劑的 57% 水平，等於把已驗證的重症保護機制重新接上。

Inference: with R21 at USD 2.99 per dose and an unchanged delivery structure, vaccine purchase outlay per child falls by roughly USD 8 across four doses (4 x USD 2.01). Gavi puts the negotiation's value at up to USD 90 million saved, 30 million extra doses, enough to protect nearly 7 million more children within five years.

Inference: closing the booster gap returns more than widening first-dose coverage. Kenya's fourth dose reached only 24% of the eligible population; lifting it to the third dose's 57% reconnects a severe-malaria protection mechanism that has already been demonstrated.

### 簡評 / Commentary
可信度：三國叢集隨機試點四年評估（The Lancet, 2026）為最強證據，但本次未取得該刊全文，只取得 WHO 摘要與期刊索引交叉引述；13% 全因死亡下降、每八宗避免一宗，均屬該評估結論。爭議點：36.3% 為第三期 48 個月中位追蹤的臨床效力，32% 為現場兩年的重症入院率降幅，兩者口徑不同，不可直接相減或並列為「效力」。缺口：未取得 2026 年 Lancet 四年評估的完整方法與信賴區間，亦未取得加強劑覆蓋率與死亡下降的劑量反應分析；喀麥隆等非隨機觀察研究調整後為 null，顯示推廣階段仍需更強的現場設計。

Confidence: the four-year cluster-randomised pilot evaluation in The Lancet, 2026 is the strongest evidence, but the full text was unreachable, so the 13% mortality drop and one-in-eight figure rest on the WHO summary cross-checked against the journal index; both are that evaluation's conclusions. Contested point: 36.3% is clinical efficacy over a median 48-month phase 3 follow-up, while 32% is a two-year reduction in severe-malaria admissions in the field. The two are different quantities and must not be subtracted or listed side by side as efficacy. Gaps: no complete methods or confidence intervals from the 2026 Lancet paper, no dose-response analysis linking booster coverage to the mortality drop, and non-randomised field studies such as Cameroon's came back null after adjustment, showing that rollout-phase designs still need strengthening.
