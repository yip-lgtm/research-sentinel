## 標題：把氯換走能救幾多人？飲用水消毒副產物的劑量鏈、可歸因病例與轉換代價
# Title: Switching away from chlorine: the DBP dose-response chain, attributable cases and the cost of the switch
（2026-11-03 21:00 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic
美國每年約 79,000 例膀胱癌新發病中，約 8,000 例可能被飲用水消毒副產物歸因。本報告追問：這數字靠甚麼量化鏈撐住、換走氯消毒能否真的攔下、代價是甚麼。範圍限美國法規與同行評審證據。
About 8,000 of 79,000 new US bladder cancer cases a year may be attributable to drinking-water disinfection byproducts. What supports that number, would switching prevent those cases, and at what cost? US regulation and peer-reviewed evidence only.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- 【機制】副產物不是氯本身，而是氯跟水裡的東西反應出來：Cl₂ 水解成 HOCl，HOCl 遇溴離子變成親電性更強的 HOBr；兩者鹵化天然有機物生成 THM 與 HAA。溴碘比例越高產物越偏溴代碘代，EPA 因此把 BDCM、溴仿、DCAA 的 MCLG 設為 0（氯仿 0.07 mg/L）。
Not chlorine but what chlorine makes with what is in the water: Cl2 hydrolyses to HOCl, HOCl plus bromide becomes the more electrophilic HOBr, and both halogenate natural organic matter into THMs and HAAs. More bromide and iodide shifts the mix toward brominated species, hence MCLG 0 for BDCM, bromoform, DCAA.
- 【機制】劑量到風險有兩段鏈。第一段：6 個美歐 case–control（2,806 病例／5,254 對照）合併得 THM4 斜率 0.00427（95% CI 0.00164–0.00626），即每升多 10 µg/L 膀胱癌 OR 約 1.044。第二段：套上 SYR3 2011 實測分布（42 州、2.694 億人），得每年約 8,000 例可歸因，74% 落在服務逾 10,000 人的地表水廠。
Two links. Dose-response: six pooled US/EU case-control studies (2,806 cases, 5,254 controls) give a THM4 slope of 0.00427 (95% CI 0.00164-0.00626), OR 1.044 per 10 ug/L. Distribution: applied to SYR3 2011 monitoring (42 states, 269.4 million people), it yields about 8,000 attributable cases a year, 74% in large surface-water plants.
- 【敏感度】結論只在窄範圍內成立。用斜率下限 0.00164，每 10 µg/L 的 OR 由 1.044 降到 1.016，歸因病例數明顯回落；EPA 自己的 population attributable fraction 由 Stage 1 前 17.1% 降至 Stage 1 後 11.5%，用 SYR3 再算更低；Stage 2 分析明言因果未確立、風險下界可低至零。且溴化副產物與 THM4 反向相關，低暴露組（<5 µg/L 參考）可能已帶毒性更高的溴系暴露。
Holds only in a narrow range. At the lower CI bound 0.00164, OR per 10 ug/L falls from 1.044 to 1.016 and the count drops; EPA's own attributable fraction fell from 17.1% pre-Stage 1 to 11.5% post-Stage 1, lower still with SYR3; the Stage 2 analysis says causality is not established and the lower bound could approach zero. Brominated byproducts vary inversely with THM4, so the low-exposure group may already carry more toxic bromine.
- 【替代假設】換消毒劑不是把風險換成零。Stage 2 經濟分析：受規管用戶年成本平均增 $0.62，需增設處理的 1,016 萬戶平均 $5.53、第 90 百分位 $10.04；服務不足 10,000 人的小型地表水廠平均 $43.05、第 90 百分位 $173.53。改氯胺則把 THM 換成 NDMA：IRIS 10⁻⁶ 風險水平 0.7 ng/L、加州目標 3 ng/L，而美國至今無強制 nitrosamine 限值。
Switching does not swap one risk for zero. Annual household cost rose $0.62 on average, but $5.53 average and $10.04 at the 90th percentile for the 10.2 million households needing added treatment, and $43.05 average and $173.53 at the 90th percentile for small plants. Chloramine trades THMs for NDMA: IRIS 1e-6 level 0.7 ng/L, California goal 3 ng/L, no federal limit.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
- 【推論】按 SYR3 2011 實測，THM4 平均介乎 40–80 µg/L 的系統服務 7,570 萬人，≤40 µg/L 的一層服務 1.154 億人——人數少三成卻承擔主要可歸因負荷。若換消毒劑要回收病例，回報最大的是大型地表水廠，小廠反而最貴（第 90 百分位每戶 $173.53）。
On SYR3 2011 monitoring, systems averaging 40-80 ug/L THM4 serve 75.7 million people while those at or below 40 ug/L serve 115.4 million, so a smaller population carries most of the burden. Large plants capture the most recoverable cases; small ones face the highest unit cost.
- 消毒這一環不能只談化學：1993 年密爾沃基 Cryptosporidium 爆發，淨水廠濁度 3 月 23 日異常上升、4 月 9 日關廠，五縣約 40.3 萬人腹瀉，疾病總成本 9,620 萬美元（醫療 3,170 萬、產能損失 6,460 萬）。荷蘭臭氧評估顯示 C. parvum 中位感染風險 10⁻³ person-year，臭氧化使基線風險降約 7 倍，bromate 誘發腎細胞癌的損失被 >10 倍健康效益抵銷。
Disinfection cannot be discussed in chemical terms alone. In the 1993 Milwaukee Cryptosporidium outbreak, plant turbidity rose abnormally on 23 March, the plant closed on 9 April, about 403,000 people across five counties developed diarrhoea and the illness cost $96.2 million. A Dutch ozone assessment put median Cryptosporidium risk at 1e-3 person-year, with ozonation cutting baseline risk seven-fold and bromate's cancer loss outweighed more than ten-fold by health gains.
- 【推論】可攔下的病例數落在「數千到接近零」的寬區間：上限是約 8,000 例，下限因 Stage 2 自述「下界可至零」而大幅塌縮。穩健的理據應是削減高暴露人群（>40 µg/L 段 7,570 萬人），而非聲稱能消滅 8,000 例。
The preventable count spans a wide band, from several thousand toward zero. The defensible justification is cutting exposure in the 75.7 million above 40 ug/L, not claiming 8,000 cases vanish.
- 【推論】換氯胺的邊際風險要另買保險：UCMR2 在 1,198 個公共供水系統、18,098 個樣本中檢出 NDMA 的比例為 10%，MRL 2 ng/L，而美國無強制限值，等於把這批系統的 nitrosamine 暴露交由各州自訂標準。
Chloramine's marginal risk needs separate cover: UCMR2 detected NDMA in 10% of 18,098 samples across 1,198 public water systems at a 2 ng/L reporting level, and with no federal limit, that exposure falls to state standards.

### 簡評 / Commentary
可信度：膀胱癌歸因數字中等偏上，但只建立在一條劑量斜率上；斜率來自觀察性 case–control，殘餘混雜無法排除。AML 端更弱：2025 年氯仿與白血病 meta-analysis 的 AML pooled OR 1.98（1.31–3.00）實際只來自單一研究（64 例 AML、31,228 對照，暴露代理是環境空氣中的氯仿），不是飲水劑量比較。未核實：全美 THM4/HAA5 的 median 與 90 百分位、常被引用的 20,000 例 AML 與「全面改 UV」反事實、全美換氯胺成本、full-scale 廠 THM 降幅；多個 EPA 與 CDC 頁面阻擋自動存取。這些留作缺口，不用記憶填補。
Confidence: the bladder cancer figure is moderate to good but rests on one dose-response slope from observational case-control studies, where residual confounding cannot be excluded. The AML evidence is weaker: the 2025 chloroform-leukemia pooled OR of 1.98 comes from one study, with ambient air rather than drinking water as exposure proxy. Unclosed gaps stay gaps.
