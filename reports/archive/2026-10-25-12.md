### 主題 / Topic
氯酸鹽是火箭推進劑的氧化劑，卻在 2026 年重啟美國飲用水監管：20 µg/L 的 MCLG 會波及哪些供水系統？
Perchlorate is a rocket-propellant oxidizer, yet it is back under US drinking-water regulation: who is exposed at the proposed 20 µg/L MCLG?

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- 【機制】氯酸鹽與碘共用甲狀腺鈉碘同向運輸體，競爭性抑制碘攝入。碘不足時甲狀腺素下降，可損傷胎兒神經發育。
  Perchlorate competes with iodide for the thyroid sodium-iodide symporter; low iodine plus lower thyroid hormone can impair fetal neurodevelopment.
- 【替代解釋】EPA 自評碘攝取抑制只是「前驅事件」。因此須按碘營養狀態分層推論，不能用單次血中濃度直接定因果。
  EPA calls iodide-uptake inhibition only a precursor event, so thyroid conclusions need iodine-status stratification rather than one blood concentration.
- 【敏感度】若孕婦碘充足，20 µg/L 下模型未見可量化神經發育傷害；若碘不足，同濃度已可推得甲狀腺素下降。結論只在碘充足假設成立，碘不足即翻正。
  If pregnant women are iodine-sufficient, modeled harm is negligible at 20 µg/L; if iodine-deficient, thyroid-hormone loss emerges at the same level.
- 【量化】EPA 2016 年貝葉斯暴露模型估計，全國約 103 個公共供水系統（90% CI 12–266）超過 20 µg/L，約 508 個（90% CI 247–843）超過 4 µg/L。
  EPA's 2016 Bayesian occurrence model estimates 103 public water systems (90% CI 12–266) above 20 µg/L and 508 (90% CI 247–843) above 4 µg/L.
- 【軍用來源】氯酸鹽是 AP 固體推進劑的氧化劑，用於火箭與導彈。ATSDR 引述 1994 年美國 AP 產量約 2,200 萬磅，其後無更新產量序列。
  Perchlorate is the oxidizer in ammonium-perchlorate rocket propellant; ATSDR cites an estimated 22 million lb of US output in 1994 with no newer series.
- 【現況】2026 年 1 月聯邦公報擬議規則將 MCLG 定為 0.02 mg/L，可執行 MCL 提供 20、40 或 80 µg/L 三個選項，並附監測、減緩與用戶公告義務。
  EPA's January 2026 proposed rule sets the MCLG at 0.02 mg/L, offers MCLs of 20, 40 or 80 µg/L, and adds monitoring, mitigation and notification duties.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impacts
- 【推論】若 MCL 採 20 µg/L，需監測與減緩的供水系統上界為 103 個；40 或 80 µg/L 選項會縮小受影響面，但碘不足孕婦仍留在高風險群。
  At a 20 µg/L MCL, up to 103 systems face monitoring and mitigation; higher options shrink that set but leave iodine-deficient pregnant women high-risk.
- 【案例】Tooele Army Depot（猶他州）與 China Lake 等推進劑基地屬歷史污染點；本次未取得可引用的現場濃度數字，故不作人數或成本推算。
  Tooele Army Depot and China Lake are historical propellant sites, but this run found no citable field concentrations, so no headcount or cost extrapolation is made.
- 【成本缺口】本題未找到可引用的每戶治理成本估算，不以規則層次代替成本結論。
  No citable per-household treatment-cost estimate was found, so regulatory status is not substituted for a cost conclusion.
