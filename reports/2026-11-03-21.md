## 標題：把氯換走能救幾多人？飲用水消毒副產物的劑量鏈、可歸因病例與轉換代價
# Title: Switching away from chlorine: the DBP dose-response chain, attributable cases and the cost of the switch
（2026-11-03 21:00 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic
美國公共供水每年約 79,000 例膀胱癌新發病中，約 8,000 例（10%）可能被飲用水消毒副產物歸因。本報告追問：那個數字靠甚麼量化鏈撐住、把氯消毒換成氯胺／臭氧／UV 能不能真的攔下它、代價是甚麼。範圍限於美國法規與同行評審證據，不涵蓋各國水質差異。
Roughly 8,000 of about 79,000 new US bladder cancer cases a year may be attributable to drinking-water disinfection byproducts. What supports that number, would switching disinfectant actually prevent those cases, and what would it cost? Scope is limited to US regulation and peer-reviewed evidence.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- 【機制】副產物不是氯本身，而是氯與水裡的東西反應出來：Cl₂ 水解成 HOCl，HOCl 遇溴離子變成親電性更強的 HOBr；兩者鹵化天然有機物（腐殖質、芳香酮酸），經三鹵甲基酮中間體生成 THM、經氧化裂解生成 HAA。溴碘比例越高產物越偏溴代碘代，EPA 因此把 BDCM、溴仿、DCAA 的 MCLG 設為 0（氯仿 0.07 mg/L）。
The byproducts are not chlorine itself but what chlorine makes with what is in the water: Cl2 hydrolyses to HOCl, HOCl plus bromide becomes the more electrophilic HOBr, and both halogenate natural organic matter via trihalomethyl ketones to THMs and via oxidative cleavage to HAAs. More bromide and iodide shifts the mix toward brominated and iodinated species, which is why EPA set the MCLG at 0 for BDCM, bromoform and DCAA.
- 【機制】劑量到風險有兩段鏈。第一段：6 個美歐 case–control（2,806 病例／5,254 對照）合併得 THM4 斜率 0.00427（95% CI 0.00164–0.00626），即每升多 10 µg/L，膀胱癌 OR 約 1.044。第二段：把斜率套上 SYR3 2011 實測分布（42 州、2.694 億人），得每年 79,000 例中約 8,000 例可歸因，其中 74% 落在服務人口逾 10,000 的地表水系統。
The exposure-to-risk chain has two links. First, dose-response: pooling six US and European case-control studies (2,806 cases, 5,254 controls) gives a THM4 slope of 0.00427 (95% CI 0.00164-0.00626), about OR 1.044 per 10 ug/L. Second, distribution: applying that slope to SYR3 2011 monitoring (42 states, 269.4 million people) yields roughly 8,000 attributable cases a year, 74% of them in surface-water systems serving over 10,000 people.
- 【敏感度】結論只在窄範圍內成立。改用斜率下限 0.00164，每 10 µg/L 的 OR 由 1.044 降到 1.016，歸因病例數明顯回落；EPA 自己的 population attributable fraction 由 Stage 1 前 17.1% 降至 Stage 1 後 11.5%，用 SYR3 再算更低；Stage 2 經濟分析更明言因果未確立、風險下界可低至零。且溴化副產物與 THM4 反向相關，低暴露組（<5 µg/L 為參考）可能已帶毒性更高的溴系暴露。
The conclusion holds only in a narrow range. At the lower CI bound of 0.00164, OR per 10 ug/L falls from 1.044 to 1.016 and the attributable count drops markedly; EPA's own population attributable fraction fell from 17.1% before Stage 1 to 11.5% after it, lower still with SYR3; the Stage 2 economic analysis states causality is not established and the lower risk bound could approach zero. Brominated byproducts also vary inversely with THM4, so the low-exposure reference group may already carry more toxic brominated exposure.
- 【替代假設】換消毒劑不是把風險換成零。Stage 2 經濟分析：受規管用戶年成本平均增 $0.62，需增設處理的 1,016 萬戶平均 $5.53、第 90 百分位 $10.04；服務人口不足 10,000 的小型地表水廠平均 $43.05、第 90 百分位 $173.53。改氯胺則把 THM 換成 NDMA：IRIS 10⁻⁶ 風險水平 0.7 ng/L、加州公共衛生目標 3 ng/L，而美國至今無強制 N‑nitrosamine 限值。
Switching disinfectant does not swap one risk for zero. Annual household cost rose $0.62 on average across regulated households but $5.53 average and $10.04 at the 90th percentile for the 10.2 million households needing added treatment, and $43.05 average and $173.53 at the 90th percentile for small surface-water plants. Chloramine trades THMs for NDMA: the IRIS 1e-6 risk level is 0.7 ng/L and the California public health goal 3 ng/L, and the US still has no enforceable nitrosamine limit.
