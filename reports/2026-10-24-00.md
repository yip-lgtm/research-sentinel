## 標題：同一個 RCM 數值，背後可能差 3.4 倍現場壽命
# Title: The Same RCM Number Can Hide a 3.4× Field-Durability Gap
（2026-10-24 00:01 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic
港珠澳大橋等海工工程以 RCM 加速氯離子試驗把關耐久。本報告回答：同一個 RCM 數值，現場壽命可差幾多？證據取自十年間海邊暴露數據，並量化機制與工程代價。
Marine works such as the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge control durability with accelerated chloride testing (RCM). This report asks how much field lifespan can hide behind a single RCM number, using ten-year coastal exposure data to quantify both the mechanism and the engineering cost.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- **【事實】** 湛江海邊暴露 10 年：RCM 高估自然擴散約 2.1–4.0 倍（飛灰、礦渣混凝土），純水泥僅約 1.1 倍。
  Ten-year Zhanjiang coastal exposure: RCM overstates natural diffusion by roughly 2.1–4.0× for fly-ash and slag concrete, but only about 1.1× for plain cement.
- **【機制】** 10% NaCl 電場加速，趕不上飛灰與礦渣的二次水化。12 年時膠孔已佔總孔過半，RCM 卻只測 28–56 天。
  A 10% NaCl electric field accelerates chloride faster than the secondary hydration of fly ash and slag can densify the matrix. At 12 years gel pores already exceed half the total pore volume, yet RCM runs for only 28–56 days.
- **【機制】** 同一數據中，F30 與 A03 的 RCM 幾乎相同（7.28 對 7.38），現場值卻是 1.85 對 6.31，差 3.4 倍。排除鹽水或試件差異：同站同水灰比下純水泥 kt≈0.9，且 56 天更貼近自然值。
  Within the same dataset, F30 and A03 have near-identical RCM values (7.28 vs 7.38) yet field values of 1.85 vs 6.31 — a 3.4× gap. Brine or specimen differences are ruled out: at the same site and water-cement ratio, plain cement gives kt≈0.9, and 56 days sits closer to the natural value.
- **【敏感度】** Fick 關係下壽命與 D 成反比：代入 RCM 原始值會令壽命預測偏短 2.1–4.0 倍；改用 56 天 kt 則偏短 2.3–3.3 倍。寒冷地區 kt 未標定，不可外推。
  Under the Fick relation, service life varies inversely with D: feeding in raw RCM values shortens predicted life by 2.1–4.0×, while using the 56-day kt still shortens it by 2.3–3.3×. kt is uncalibrated for cold climates and cannot be extrapolated there.
- **【事實】** 港珠澳大橋 55 公里、施工 9 年、壽命 120 年，RCM 為標準耐久品質把關方法。
  The Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge spans 55 km, took 9 years to build, and is designed for 120 years; RCM is the standard durability quality-control method.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
- **【事實】** 案例：克爾克島橋下 18 根柱暴露 52 個月，鹽度 3.5%。碳鋼浪濤區年均截面損失 34 μm，點蝕速率 370 μm/年；乾濕區更達 550 μm/年。
  Case: 18 bridge pier columns exposed for 52 months at 3.5% salinity. Carbon steel in the splash zone lost 34 μm cross-section per year, with pitting at 370 μm/year; the tidal zone reached 550 μm/year.
- **【推論】** 同一 RCM 門檻會把現場性能差 3.4 倍的配比視為同等，低碳飛灰與礦渣的耐久優勢被抹平，或反推更高水泥用量。
  A single RCM threshold treats mixes whose field performance differs by 3.4× as equivalent, erasing the durability advantage of low-carbon fly ash and slag, or pushing back toward higher cement content.
- **【推論】** 120 年壽命若只依 RCM 原始值，品質追溯鏈缺少現場轉換因子。本題未找到橋樑「預測 vs 實測壽命」的公開驗證，不能斷言該橋不安全。
  If a 120-year design life relies only on raw RCM values, the quality traceability chain lacks a field conversion factor. No public verification of predicted versus measured bridge life was found, so no claim of unsafety is made.
- **【事實】** 美國逾半橋樑為鋼筋混凝土，橋樑腐蝕直接年成本 59–97 億美元，含間接成本可高 10 倍（2007 年估，數據偏舊）。
  More than half of US bridges are reinforced concrete; direct annual bridge corrosion cost is USD 5.9–9.7 billion, up to 10× with indirect costs (2007 estimate, dated).
