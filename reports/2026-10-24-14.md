## 標題：高氯酸鹽：EPA 說「不構成公共健康疑慮」卻被迫設上限，軍方自有的 24 ppb 觸發值會被怎樣改寫
# Title: Perchlorate: EPA says it is no public-health concern yet must set limits — and what happens to the military's own 24 ppb trigger
（2026-10-24 14:00 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic
高氯酸鹽是固體火箭推進劑的氧化劑，美國九成國內產量為國防航太而造。本報告回答：2026 年 1 月 EPA 的飲用水規則同時聲稱高氯酸鹽「不太可能以公共健康關注的水平出現」，又因法院裁決被迫提出強制上限。這個矛盾在科學與成本上如何成立，又如何改寫軍方設施的實際義務。

Perchlorate is the oxidizer in solid rocket propellant, and 90% of US-produced perchlorate is made for defence and aerospace. This report asks how EPA's January 2026 drinking-water proposal can call perchlorate not a public-health concern and still propose enforceable limits, and what that does to the practical obligations of military installations and affected communities.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- **【機制】** 高氯酸鹽不破壞甲狀腺，而是競爭性抑制鈉／碘轉運蛋白（NIS），阻碘入腺，T4 降、TSH 升。效應由體內碘量調節，缺碘孕婦的胎兒最脆弱。EPA 2005 年 RfD 0.7 µg/kg/day 以 7 µg/kg/day 的抑制碘攝取無效應水平為基礎，屬前驅效應而非疾病終點。
  EN: Perchlorate does not damage the thyroid; it competitively inhibits the sodium/iodide symporter, blocking iodide uptake so T4 falls and TSH rises. Effect size is modulated by iodine status, and fetuses of iodine-deficient pregnant women are most vulnerable. EPA's 2005 RfD of 0.7 µg/kg/day rests on a 7 µg/kg/day NOEL for inhibited iodide uptake — a precursor endpoint, not a disease endpoint.
- **【事實】** 2026-01-06 擬議規則（91 FR 398）明文稱高氯酸鹽不太可能以公共健康關注水平出現，但 D.C. 巡迴法院 NRDC v. Regan（67 F.4th 397）撤銷 EPA 撤回 2011 年管制決定，理由是 SDWA 不授權撤回，且禁止以非 NPDWR 結案。EPA 仍須提出 MCLG 20 µg/L 與 20／40／80 µg/L 三個可執行上限。
  EN: The proposed rule of 2026-01-06 (91 FR 398) states that perchlorate is not likely to occur at levels of public-health concern, but the D.C. Circuit in NRDC v. Regan (67 F.4th 397) vacated EPA's withdrawal of the 2011 determination, holding SDWA does not authorize withdrawal and barring any action other than an NPDWR. EPA therefore proposes an MCLG of 20 µg/L and co-proposes three enforceable MCLs: 20, 40 and 80 µg/L.
- **【事實】** 貨幣化淨效益在三個上限、兩種貼現率下全為負：20 µg/L 為 −780 萬（3%）與 −1730 萬美元（7%）；80 µg/L 為 −330 萬與 −990 萬。替代解釋「不划算即應撤回」被排除——撤回已被法院禁止，且 SDWA 第 1412(b)(1)(A) 要求評估非貨幣化效益。80 µg/L 最接近平衡但仍為負。
  EN: Monetized net benefits are negative for every option and discount rate: at 20 µg/L, −$7.8M at 3% and −$17.3M at 7%; at 80 µg/L, −$3.3M and −$9.9M. The rival explanation "too costly, so drop it" is excluded, because withdrawal was already barred by the court and SDWA §1412(b)(1)(A) requires non-monetized benefits to be assessed. The 80 µg/L option comes closest to breakeven yet stays negative.
- **【事實】** GAO 2005 年盤點：全美 395 個場所、153 個公共供水系統檢出高氯酸鹽，濃度 4 ppb 至逾 370 萬 ppb，約 28% 源於國防航太推進劑製造、靶場試射或廢炸藥處置。國防部已在 258 個現役設施取樣，至 FY2006 花約 8800 萬美元研究經費，其中 6000 萬用於處理技術。
  EN: GAO's 2005 inventory records perchlorate at 395 U.S. sites including 153 public water systems, at 4 ppb to over 3.7 million ppb, with about 28% of sites traced to defence or aerospace propellant manufacture, test firing or explosives disposal. DoD had sampled 258 active installations and spent about $88M on perchlorate research through FY2006, $60M of it on treatment technologies.
- **【敏感度】** 兩條邊界。貼現率由 3% 升至 7% 令 20 µg/L 淨負值擴大逾一倍，但三個選項在兩種費率下皆為負，方向不變。若改用 EFSA 2014 的 TDI 0.3 µg/kg/day（比 EPA RfD 嚴 2.3 倍），隱含健康水平降至約 10.5 ppb，受影響系統數會明顯上升；「僅約 100 個系統超標」只在採用 EPA RfD 時成立。
  EN: Two boundaries. Raising the discount rate from 3% to 7% more than doubles the 20 µg/L net loss, but all three options stay negative at both rates, so the direction is unchanged. Using EFSA's 2014 TDI of 0.3 µg/kg/day instead, 2.3 times stricter than EPA's RfD, implies a health-based level near 10.5 ppb and a materially larger number of affected systems. The "only about 100 systems exceed" conclusion therefore holds only under EPA's RfD.
