# 主題

**中文**

**電池儲能已便宜到足以在晚高峰取代天然氣發電，為何家庭與工商用電費卻沒有同步下降？** 這是 2024–2025 年電力市場最被誤解的一件事：BloombergNEF 記錄到固定式電池 pack 價格降至每 kWh 70 美元、全球四小時電池項目成本跌至每 MWh 78 美元的歷史新低；同期美國電網已安裝逾 43 GW 儲能，加州晚高峰實際放電量增加約五倍並確實排擠燃氣。但「儲能變便宜」與「帳單變便宜」之間，隔著零售電價的季節平均計價方式、系統資產成本分攤規則、電池的全生命週期成本與放電效率。本報告只回答這一個問題：儲能的成本紅利去了哪裡、誰真正拿到。

**English**

**Battery storage has become cheap enough to displace natural-gas generation at the evening peak — so why have household and business electricity bills not fallen in step?** This is the most misunderstood claim in power markets since 2024–2025. BloombergNEF records stationary battery pack prices falling to USD 70/kWh and a global four-hour battery project benchmark of USD 78/MWh, both record lows, while the US grid has installed more than 43 GW of storage and California evening peak discharge has risen roughly fivefold, actually displacing gas generation. Yet between "storage got cheaper" and "bills got cheaper" sit retail tariffs that price on seasonal averages, the cost-allocation rules for grid assets, batteries' full lifecycle cost and round-trip efficiency. This report answers exactly one question: where did the storage cost dividend go, and who actually received it?

# 核心發現

**中文**

**一、成本交叉點確實出現，但口徑是「項目成本」而非零售電價。** BNEF 2025 年 12 月資料：固定式儲能 pack 價格降至 70 美元/kWh（年減 45%），全品項平均 pack 價格 108 美元/kWh（較 2024 年降 8%），全球四小時電池項目 benchmark 成本年減 27% 至 78 美元/MWh；同期新建 combined-cycle gas turbine（CCGT，聯合循環燃氣渦輪機）的 levelized cost of electricity（平準化度電成本，LCOE）上升 16% 至 102 美元/MWh，為有紀錄以來最高。

**二、部署已不是邊際事件。** EIA：2024 年新增 10.4 GW 電池（僅占美國 1,230 GW utility-scale 發電裝機的 2%），2025 年再增 15 GW（歷史新高），2025 年底運轉容量 43.6 GW，2026 上半年再加 8.3 GW、逼近 52 GW。實際替代已發生：加州 2022 年 5–6 月晚高峰電池放電平均不到 1 GW，2025 年升至 4.9 GW，EIA 明言「displacing natural gas generation」；ERCOT 2025 年夏季 8 點時段電池平均供應 4 GW，天然氣發電占比由 2023、2024 前九個月的 47% 降至 43%。

**三、但「pack 價格」不是取代 peaker 的決策價格。** EIA 對獨立 150 MW／600 MWh BESS 參考機組給出的 overnight capital cost（隔夜資本成本）為 1,744 美元/kW（折合 436 美元/kWh），另計 fixed O&M（固定維護費）40 美元/kW-yr。NREL 2025 ATB 假設 roundtrip efficiency（往返效率）85%，且 FOM 內含使電池維持額定容量至 15 年壽命的 augmentation（容量增強）成本，為資本成本的 4%。Cole 等人（NREL, 2025）以 bottom-up 模型估出的四小時系統總資本成本，2024 年起點為 334 美元/kWh，2035 年依三條軌線降至 147／234／339 美元/kWh。

**四、電費沒降下來，是因為計價機制把紅利吸收在別處。** EIA：多數零售用戶按供電成本的「seasonal average（季節平均）」計價，因此看不到日間價格波動。PNNL-39073（美國能源部，2026 年 3 月）則從資產側解釋：成本會被平均攤入用戶費率，「customers are paying for all those assets year-round, even if they are only being used for a few hours」；報告並明言節省金額「depends heavily on the electricity rate structure」。其引用的 MISO 模型（11 GW 電池）預估 2025–2035 年可省 4.5 billion 美元電力成本、尖峰日價由 245.30 降至 86.90 美元/MWh；已實現案例包括德州 2024 年 1 月電池釋出 3 GW 燃氣、節省 750 million 美元日前市場成本，以及 Nantucket 6 MW／48 MWh 電池（約 5,000 萬美元）取代原估 2 億美元以上海底電纜。

**【推論】一個完整四小時循環的真實能量帳。** 85% 往返效率意味著輸出 600 MWh 需輸入約 706 MWh，損失約 106 MWh。若充電電能以 PNNL 引用的 2024 年全時段平均批發價 24.21 美元/MWh 取得，單次循環的購電成本約 17,100 美元，即每日一循環約 624 萬美元／年、每日兩循環約 1,250 萬美元／年，已足以與 150 MW 機組 600 萬美元／年的 FOM 相當或更高——這還未計折舊、容量衰退與互聯費用。反之，若充電來自原本會被棄發的太陽能，邊際購電成本趨近於零，這正解释了加州案例為何能實現實質排擠：**同一顆電池，是「負載移轉器」還是「浪費 15% 電能的資產」，取決於它充的是什麼電。**

**English**

**First, the cost crossover is real, but it is a project-cost comparison, not a retail-bill comparison.** BloombergNEF (December 2025): stationary battery pack prices fell to USD 70/kWh (down 45% year-on-year), average pack prices across segments to USD 108/kWh (down 8% versus 2024), and the global benchmark cost for a four-hour battery project fell 27% to USD 78/MWh; over the same period, the levelized cost of electricity (LCOE) for new-build combined-cycle gas turbines (CCGTs) rose 16% to USD 102/MWh, the highest on record.

**Second, deployment is no longer marginal.** EIA: 10.4 GW of battery capacity was added in 2024 (only 2% of the 1,230 GW of US utility-scale generating capacity), a record 15 GW was added in 2025, operational capacity reached 43.6 GW by end-2025, and another 8.3 GW was added in the first half of 2026, approaching 52 GW. Real displacement is documented: California evening-peak battery discharge rose from under 1 GW (May–June 2022) to 4.9 GW in 2025, which EIA explicitly states was "displacing natural gas generation"; in ERCOT, batteries supplied an average 4 GW in the 8 p.m. hour in summer 2025, and the natural-gas share of generation fell from 47% in the first nine months of 2023 and 2024 to 43% in the first nine months of 2025.

**Third, however, pack price is not the decision price for replacing a peaker.** EIA's reference design for a standalone 150 MW / 600 MWh BESS carries an overnight capital cost of USD 1,744/kW (equivalent to USD 436/kWh) plus fixed O&M of USD 40/kW-yr. NREL's 2025 ATB assumes an 85% roundtrip efficiency and folds battery augmentation — keeping the system at rated capacity across a 15-year life — into FOM set at 4% of capital cost. Cole et al. (NREL, 2025), using a bottom-up model, put the total capital cost of a four-hour system at a 2024 starting point of USD 334/kWh, declining along three trajectories to USD 147 / 234 / 339 per kWh in 2035.

**Fourth, bills did not fall because the pricing mechanism absorbs the dividend elsewhere.** EIA notes most retail consumers pay on the seasonal average cost of providing electricity and therefore never see daily price fluctuations. PNNL-39073 (US Department of Energy, March 2026) explains the asset-side mechanism: costs are averaged into customer rates, so "customers are paying for all those assets year-round, even if they are only being used for a few hours," and the report states explicitly that savings "depends heavily on the electricity rate structure." The MISO model it cites (11 GW of batteries) projects up to USD 4.5 billion in power-cost savings over 2025–2035 and a peak daily price falling from USD 245.30 to USD 86.90/MWh. Documented real-world cases include Texas in January 2024, where batteries released 3 GW of gas and saved USD 750 million in day-ahead market costs, and Nantucket, where a 6 MW / 48 MWh battery costing about USD 50 million displaced a submarine cable estimated at more than USD 200 million.

**【推論 / Inference】The energy ledger of one full four-hour cycle.** An 85% roundtrip efficiency means delivering 600 MWh requires roughly 706 MWh of input, losing about 106 MWh. If that charging energy is bought at the 2024 all-hours average wholesale price of USD 24.21/MWh cited by PNNL, a single full cycle costs about USD 17,100 to buy — roughly USD 6.24 million per year at one cycle per day, or USD 12.5 million at two — already on par with or above the USD 6 million per year FOM for a 150 MW system, before depreciation, capacity fade and interconnection costs. If instead the charging energy is surplus solar that would otherwise be curtailed, the marginal purchase cost approaches zero, which is precisely why the California case produces real displacement: **whether a given battery is a load-shifting asset or one that wastes 15% of its energy depends on what electricity it is charged with.**

# 潛在影響

**中文**

**對家庭用戶與高電費用戶：** 不要把 pack 價格下跌直接換算成電費預期。PNNL 的案例顯示，真正立刻受益的是**高 demand charge（需量費）**的用戶——Kaiser Permanente Richmond Medical Center 的夏季電費有高達 45% 是需量費，安裝 1 MW／1 MWh 系統後每年減免達 394,000 美元。對分攤式零售電價的一般住戶，儲能的紅利須經由「少建 peaker／少建輸配電」兩道成本分攤與費率審定才會出現，時滯以年計。

**【推論】對公用事業與監管機關：** 儲能採購應比較的是全生命週期成本（USD 334–436/kWh 安裝資本 + 4%/yr FOM/增強 + 互聯 + 循環壽命 + 折現），不是 70 美元/kWh 的 pack 報價。2026 年美國計畫新增 24 GW 電池；若利用率低於預期而燃氣仍待命，資產將被閒置但成本已攤入費率——這是採購時最需要壓力測試的風險。

**【推論】對排放與能源安全：** EIA 明言電池是 secondary source（次能源），不創造能源，只搬移能源；85% 往返效率意味每充放一次就多買約 15% 電能。若這部分電能來自燃氣，局部排放反而可能上升；只有在充電電能來自棄發光電、且被替代的確實是燃氣時，才有淨減碳。PNNL 與 EIA 均未量化此項，這是本次研究最重要的證據缺口。

**對產業競爭力與決策：** 天然氣 peaker 約占美國裝機 19%、卻只貢獻 2021 年淨發電量的 3.1%（約 1,000 座），卻支撐著 2024 年每 MWh 342.05 美元（全時段平均 24.21 美元）的尖峰批發價格。電池在尖峰時段的相對經濟性改善，會實質改變資料中心、製造業與住宅的用電成本結構——但前提是費率設計允許儲能容量被正確計價與分攤。

**English**

**For households and high-tariff users:** do not translate falling pack prices directly into expected bill savings. PNNL's cases show that the immediate beneficiaries are customers with high demand charges — at Kaiser Permanente's Richmond Medical Center, demand charges made up as much as 45% of the summer bill, and a 1 MW / 1 MWh system reduced demand charges by up to USD 394,000 per year. For residential customers on averaged retail tariffs, the storage dividend must first pass through two filters — avoided peaker and avoided transmission/distribution capital — and then through rate cases, a lag measured in years.

**【推論 / Inference】For utilities and regulators:** storage procurement should compare full lifecycle cost (USD 334–436/kWh installed capital, plus 4%/yr FOM and augmentation, interconnection, cycle life and discounting), not the USD 70/kWh pack quote. US developers plan to add 24 GW of batteries in 2026; if utilisation disappoints while gas remains on standby, the asset sits idle while its cost has already been spread into rates — the risk that most needs stress-testing at the point of purchase.

**【推論 / Inference】For emissions and energy security:** EIA is explicit that batteries are secondary sources that shift rather than create energy, and an 85% roundtrip efficiency means buying roughly 15% more energy per cycle. If that incremental energy is gas-fired, local emissions could rise; net decarbonisation arises only when charging comes from curtailed solar and the displaced resource is genuinely gas. Neither PNNL nor EIA quantifies this, which is the most important evidence gap in this study.

**For industrial competitiveness and decision-making:** gas peakers represent about 19% of US installed capacity and roughly 1,000 plants, yet only 3.1% of 2021 net generation, and they underpin the 2024 peak-hour wholesale price of USD 342.05/MWh against an all-hours average of USD 24.21. Batteries' improving relative economics at peak hours materially change the cost structure for data centres, manufacturers and households — but only if tariff design allows storage capacity to be valued and allocated correctly.
