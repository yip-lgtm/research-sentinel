### 主題 / Topic

3D 列印金屬件疲勞失效，表面粗糙度與內部孔隙何者主導？HIP 與表面精加工各挽回多少壽命？低成本顯微 CT 能否普及醫療件缺陷檢測？

For additively manufactured metal parts, does surface roughness or internal porosity dominate fatigue failure? How much life do hot isostatic pressing (HIP) and surface finishing each recover? Can low-cost micro-CT make defect inspection routine for medical parts?

### 核心發現 / Key Findings

【機制】疲勞由表面或內部缺陷起裂，兩者競爭壽命。
【機制】HIP 高壓高溫閉合孔隙、抑制起裂，卻不改善表面。
排除替代解釋：HIP 優勢若只屬熱處理，資料庫對照不支持。
排除替代解釋：離散若只屬噪音，統一縮放後應消失，實則仍見系統偏差。
FatigueData-AM2022 收 1,848 篇、116 種合金、15,146 個 S-N 點。
65% 數據供高周疲勞安全壽命設計，屬安全關鍵路徑。

【Mechanism】Fatigue cracks initiate at surface or internal defects, so the two compete for life.
【Mechanism】HIP closes internal pores under high pressure and temperature and suppresses initiation, but does not improve the surface.
Alternative explanation rejected: if HIP's advantage were only heat treatment, the database's HIP-versus-heat-treatment comparison would not support it.
Alternative explanation rejected: if the scatter were pure noise, it should vanish after unified scaling; systematic deviation persists.
FatigueData-AM2022 holds 1,848 articles, 116 alloy types and 15,146 S–N points.
65% of the data serve high-cycle fatigue safe-life design, a safety-critical path.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact

【替代假設】自製 CT 造價 10,725 美元；商用 26 萬至 75 萬美元以上。
【敏感度】每日 10 次、每年 250 日、五年 12,500 次，約 0.86 美元/次。
【敏感度】若每日 1 次，五年 1,250 次，約 8.58 美元/次。
【推論】300 美元零件的 30 美元檢測門檻，兩情境皆未觸及。
具體案例：PLOS ONE 用該機檢測鈦 3D 列印醫療件內部缺陷。
缺口：未含人力、校準與 X 射線管更換，亦缺植入失效率數據。

【Alternative assumption】The DIY scanner costs USD 10,725; commercial units run USD 260,000 to over USD 750,000.
【Sensitivity】At 10 scans per day, 250 workdays per year and five years, 12,500 scans cost about USD 0.86 each.
【Sensitivity】At 1 scan per day, 1,250 scans over five years cost about USD 8.58 each.
【Inference】The paper's rule of thumb caps scanning of a USD 300 part at USD 30; neither scenario reaches that limit.
Concrete case: PLOS ONE used this scanner to detect internal defects in titanium 3D-printed medical parts.
Gap: labour, calibration and X-ray tube replacement are excluded, and no implant failure-rate data were obtained.

### 簡評 / Commentary

證據強度：機制有期刊與 NASA 一手報告，但疲勞證據屬文献彙整，非單一對照試驗。NASA 表面粗糙度試驗（Lambert, SLM-In718）已讀摘要，惟 HCF 折減係數與穩定連結未能擷取，故不列正式來源。缺陷檢測成本影響屬工程經濟推算，尚未連到保險給付或醫療給付規則。本輪工具回合用盡，存檔、品質閘與 git 提交未完成，故此交付屬暫行版本。

Evidence strength: the mechanism is supported by a peer-reviewed dataset paper and a NASA primary report, but the fatigue evidence is an evidence-base synthesis rather than one controlled trial. The NASA surface-finish study was read only in abstract, so its numerical knockdown factor and stable link were not cited. The inspection-cost implications remain engineering-economic inference and are not yet tied to insurance or medical reimbursement rules. The tool-call budget was exhausted before file persistence, the quality gate and the git commit could be completed, so this delivery is provisional.
