### 主題 / Topic

同一枚反車載地雷，為何有人只傷、有人卻當場死亡？本文追問土壤衝量與車體幾何如何決定生存邊界，並找出可轉移到排爆車與事故車的防護原則。

Why can the same anti-vehicle mine merely injure one crew and instantly kill another? This report traces how soil impulse and vehicle geometry set the survival boundary, and identifies design principles transferable to EOD and crash-response vehicles.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings

- 【機制】衝擊波在空／土界面反射成張力波，撕裂土帽；高壓氣體沿土體裂隙噴出，形成氣體與土屑構成的空心倒錐，再與車底互動，把壓力與衝量傳入座艙。
- The shockwave reflects at the air–soil interface into a tension wave that ruptures the soil cap; high-pressure gas vents along failure planes, forming a hollow inverse cone of gas and soil ejecta that loads the vehicle floor.
- 【機制】車體幾何決定衝量路徑：扁平底面令壓力集中並把載荷傳給乘員；V 形底盤抬高座艙、拉開距離，把爆破產物引向車身兩側。
- Geometry sets the load path: a flat floor concentrates pressure and transmits load to occupants, whereas a V-hull lifts the crew compartment, increases standoff and diverts detonation products sideways.
- 【量化】羅德西亞 676 次觸雷事故、4,874 名乘員中，42 人死亡（0.8%）、906 人受傷（18.4%）；同一來源另報告無防護輕型車死亡率約 45%、防護車約 9%，兩組口徑不同。
- Across 676 Rhodesian mine incidents, 42 of 4,874 passengers died (0.8%) and 906 were injured (18.4%); the same source separately reports roughly 45% fatality for unprotected light vehicles versus 9% for protected ones.
- 【量化】阿富汗戰事中，聯軍與當地安全部隊佔全部作戰死亡 45.6%（2,233/4,895），反車載地雷與 IED 為主因。
- In Afghanistan, coalition and local security forces accounted for 45.6% of all combat deaths (2,233/4,895), mainly from anti-vehicle mines and IEDs.
- 【替代解釋】死亡率下降同時伴隨嚴格駕駛紀律與救護改善，且歷史紀錄無對照組，難把全部效益歸因於車體。
- Lower fatality coincided with stricter driving discipline and better trauma care, and the historical series had no control group, so vehicle design alone cannot carry the causal claim.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact

- 【推論】以 4,874 人同等暴露量計，若維持 45% 死亡率對應約 2,193 例死亡；改用防護後報告的 9% 則約 439 例，差約 1,754 例。差額同含車體、紀律與救護，不能全記於車體。
- On the same 4,874 exposures, a 45% fatality rate implies about 2,193 deaths versus roughly 439 at the reported protected-vehicle 9%, a gap near 1,754 deaths that also embeds discipline and medical-care effects.
- 【具體案例】羅德西亞 1972–1980 布希戰爭改裝車隊：676 次事故、0.8% 死亡、18.4% 受傷，可逐項回溯原始 PDF。
- The modified Rhodesian fleets of the 1972–1980 Bush War are the clearest auditable case here: 676 incidents, 0.8% fatality and 18.4% injury, traceable item by item to the source PDF.
- 【推論】V 形底盤、抬高座艙、剛性艙段與乘員約束同樣適用排爆車、消防車與事故車，屬可轉移工程收益。
- V-hulls, raised crew cells, monocoques and restraints also serve EOD, fire and crash-response vehicles, making the benefit transferable engineering rather than a single-service verdict.
- 【敏感度】若 45%→9% 主要來自更快救護，車體可歸因降幅會遠小於表面數字；把 45.6% 推廣到平民日常亦不成立，因暴露頻率不同。結論僅適用於近底引爆、乘員留在座艙的情境。
- Sensitivity: if much of the 45%→9% gain came from faster medical response, the design-attributable reduction is far smaller; extending 45.6% to civilian daily life also fails because exposure differs. Conclusions hold only for near-underbody detonation with occupants in the cell.

### 簡評 / Commentary

證據等級：中。機制與數字可在原始 PDF 逐字核對，但數據來自 1970 年代戰地紀錄，且防護與駕駛紀律同時改變，45%→9% 應視為關聯而非純因果。本題未找到現代對照實測與可靠的平民年度死亡數字，故不作全球規模估算。

Evidence grade: medium. The mechanism and figures are verbatim-checkable, but the Rhodesian series is a 1970s field record without controls, so the 45%→9% shift is association, not clean causality. No modern controlled comparison or reliable annual civilian casualty series was verifiable, so no global estimate is attempted.
