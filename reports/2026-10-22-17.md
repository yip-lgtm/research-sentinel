## 標題：美國職業熱害防護：聯邦執法强度與熱死亡負擔的落差
# Title: US Occupational Heat Protection: The Gap Between Federal Enforcement and Measured Heat Mortality
（2026-10-22 17:25 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic
本報告回答：美國聯邦職業熱害防護，是否匹配其可量化的健康負擔？範圍涵蓋 OSHA 熱危害執法計畫、聯邦熱標準進度與熱死亡統計。重點是雇主成本、勞動者安全與公共衛生預算的實際落差。

This report asks one question: does US federal protection against occupational heat match the measurable health burden it is meant to prevent? The scope covers OSHA's Heat National Emphasis Program, the status of a binding federal heat standard, and official heat-mortality statistics. The answer bears on employer cost, worker safety, and public-health budgeting.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- **聯邦標準仍未落地。** 熱傷害與疾病預防規則在聯邦公報仍屬「Proposed Rule」，2025-09-25 僅延長聽證後意見期；截至 2026-10-22，OSHA 未發布最終熱標準。
- **2026 年專案刪去量化檢查目標。** 2022 年指令要求各區域把熱檢查提高至 2017–2021 基線的 100%；2026-04-10 生效的 CPL 03-00-024 改稱以執法、外展與合規協助達成。
- **目標產業名單收縮。** 附錄按 2021–2024 數據重訂，移除 46 個、加入 22 個、保留 33 個，合計 55 個目標產業。
- **官方數字是下限。** OSHA 引 BLS 指 2021–2024 年平均每年 48 宗職業熱死亡、3,793 宗缺勤或受限個案，並稱未反映真實規模；PLOS Climate 指 1999–2023 年官方錄逾 20,000 宗熱死亡，CDC 數字應視為最低估計。
- **州級規範提供可稽核門檻。** 加州 §3395 規定 80°F 以上須備遮蔭、每人每小時一夸脫飲水、95°F 以上啟動高溫程序。

- **No binding federal standard.** OSHA's heat rule remains classified as a "Proposed Rule"; the September 25, 2025 document only extended the post-hearing comment period. No final OSHA heat rule was published in 2026 as of October 22, 2026.
- **The 2026 emphasis program dropped its numeric goal.** The 2022 directive required each region to raise heat inspections 100% above the 2017–2021 baseline. The April 10, 2026 CPL 03-00-024 instead states the goal is achieved "by a combination of enforcement (which includes inspection targeting), outreach to employers, and compliance assistance."
- **The target industry list contracted.** The April 2026 appendix rebuilt the list from 2021–2024 BLS and OSHA data, removing 46 industries, adding 22, retaining 33, for 55 total.
- **Official counts are a floor.** OSHA cites BLS averaging 48 heat-related workplace deaths and 3,793 DART cases per year in 2021–2024, adding these likely miss the true magnitude. A 2025 PLOS Climate study reports over 20,000 US heat deaths officially reported for 1999–2023 and calls CDC figures minimum estimates.
- **A state benchmark shows what binding law looks like.** California Title 8 §3395 requires shade above 80°F, one quart of water per employee per hour, and high-heat procedures at 95°F or above.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
- 【推論】聯邦規則長期停留在提案，雇主可能無聯邦層面的供水、遮蔭與休息義務，補救只靠一般職責條款，成本與可預測性低於州級硬性規範。
- 【推論】取消 100% 檢查目標並縮小至 55 個產業，可能令有限檢查資源遠離高暴露、低舉報行業，延後整改。
- 【推論】可稽核的溫度門檻（80°F 熱指數、95°F 高溫程序）對新進、臨時及未適應熱環境工人，可能比逐案查緋更能預防重症。
- 【推論】跨州營運雇主宜把加州 §3395 當作採購與合規基準，因其條款比聯邦指引更具體可稽核。

- 【推論】While the federal rule stays proposed, an employer may face no federal duty to supply water, shade, or rest; remedies fall back to the general duty clause, with lower and less predictable cost than a state standard.
- 【推論】Removing the 100% inspection target and narrowing the list to 55 industries may divert limited capacity away from high-exposure, low-reporting sectors.
- 【推論】Auditable temperature triggers are likely more protective for new, temporary, or unacclimatized workers than case-by-case inspection.
- 【推論】Employers operating across states should treat California's §3395 as a defensible procurement and compliance baseline.
