## 標題：鹹水唔止難飲：海岸鹽化點樣將安全飲用水變成健康暴露
# Title: Salty water is not just unpalatable: how coastal salinization turns "safe" drinking water into a health exposure
（2026-10-23 04:33 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic
孟加拉三項研究把飲用水鹽化連到高血壓、嬰兒死亡同升學倒退。WHO《飲用水質指引》卻明言鈉、氯化物與總溶解固體不設健康基準值。兩者點樣同時成立？答案係一條被鹽化推翻嘅暴露假設。

Three independent Bangladesh studies connect drinking-water salinity to hypertension, infant mortality, and grade advancement. WHO's drinking-water guidance nevertheless states that no health-based guideline value is established for sodium, chloride, or total dissolved solids. How can both be true? The answer lies in one exposure assumption that salinization invalidates.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- **【機制】** WHO「無健康基準值」係條件式結論：指南假設飲用水鈉通常低於 20 mg/L，並按成人每日 2 L 計。西南三區 266 戶平均 868.26 mg/L NaCl，按鈉佔 39.3% 折算約 341 mg/L 鈉；日飲 2 L 即 683 mg，佔 WHO <2,000 mg/日建議 34%。前提一失效，美學參數就變健康暴露。
- **【機制】** 味覺係第二道防線：WHO 指氯味覺門檻 200–300 mg/L、逾 250 mg/L 可察覺，卻承認部分人會習慣低水平氯味。長期暴露令退避反應減弱：鹽度越高，鈉負荷越大而味覺保護越弱。
- **【劑量—反應】** Khan 等（n=6,296）測到高血壓校正勝算比 1.650（CI 1.101–2.473）；Dasgupta 等（39,150 名兒童）把產前最後一個月鹽度由 0.52 升至 7.83 dS/m，兩月內嬰兒死亡概率升約 30%；Akter 測到 TDS >1,000 mg/L 令 7–12 歲升學概率跌 6.7 個百分點。
- **【替代解釋】** 「係窮或砷污染？」部分被排除：Dasgupta 控制母親年齡、教育、財富、廁所、水源與燃料後效應仍可比傳統變量，且只在產前最後月顯著；Khan 用混合效應模型處理家庭與群組變異。惟屬觀察研究，Khan 明言不作因果推論。
- **【敏感度】** 嬰兒死亡模型在 40 km（t=2.21，N=8,183）及 30 km（t=2.07，N=4,847）顯著，到 20 km（t=1.52，N=2,072）不顯著，故「約 30%」只在 30 km 內可靠插值時成立。高血壓 CI 下限意味風險升約 10%，上限約 147%。鈉負荷兩情境：日飲 1 L 則 683→342 mg（17%）；NaCl 為主因且 TDS ≥2,000 mg/L 可達 1,574 mg/日（79%）。維持低鈉水源則指南結論翻正。

- **【Mechanism】** WHO's "no health-based guideline value" is a conditional conclusion: the guideline assumes drinking-water sodium is typically below 20 mg/L, evaluated at a default intake of 2 L/day for adults. Across 266 households in three southwestern districts, mean NaCl was 868.26 mg/L, which at 39.3% sodium implies about 341 mg/L sodium; 2 L/day supplies ≈683 mg, roughly 34% of WHO's <2,000 mg/day recommendation. Once that premise fails, an aesthetic parameter becomes a health exposure.
- **【Mechanism】** Taste is the second guardrail: WHO places chloride taste thresholds at 200–300 mg/L and detects tastes above 250 mg/L, yet notes that some consumers become accustomed to low-level chloride taste. Chronic exposure erodes that avoidance response — the saltier the water, the higher the sodium load and the weaker the taste-based protection.
- **【Dose–response】** Khan et al. (n=6,296) report an adjusted odds ratio of 1.650 (CI 1.101–2.473) for hypertension; Dasgupta et al. (39,150 children) find that raising salinity in the final prenatal month from 0.52 to 7.83 dS/m raises under-two-month mortality probability by about 30%; Akter finds TDS >1,000 mg/L lowers grade-advancement probability for children aged 7–12 by 6.7 percentage points.
- **【Alternative explanation】** "Is this simply poverty or arsenic?" is partly excluded: Dasgupta's effect survives controls for maternal age, education, wealth, toilet, water source and cooking fuel, and appears only in the final prenatal month; Khan uses a mixed-effects model handling household and cluster variation. Both remain observational, and Khan explicitly declines causal claims.
- **【Sensitivity】** The infant-mortality model is significant at 40 km (t=2.21, N=8,183) and 30 km (t=2.07, N=4,847) but not at 20 km (t=1.52, N=2,072), so the ~30% figure holds only where salinity interpolates reliably within ~30 km. The hypertension CI implies about +10% to +147% risk. For sodium load: at 1 L/day, 683 mg falls to ≈342 mg (17%); if NaCl dominates, TDS ≥2,000 mg/L could reach ≈1,574 mg/day (79%). The guidance conclusion flips back for low-sodium sources.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
- **【推論】** Das 等量到 NaCl 每升 100 mg/L，每戶年健康成本增 BDT 262（USD 3.28）；平均 868.26 mg/L 時為 BDT 2,270/戶/年。按此斜率線性外推，減 500 mg/L 可省約 BDT 1,310/戶/年（約 USD 16；本人計算，非原文）。
- **【案例】** 西南三區十年安全供水項目淨現值約 USD 7.33M，每年省 BDT 1.70M（可支配收入約 1%），用池塘砂濾；Gabura、Munshigonj 等五聯社遷移風險最高。

- **【Inference】** Das et al. measure +BDT 262 (USD 3.28) per household per year for each additional 100 mg/L NaCl, and BDT 2,270 (USD 28.38) per household-year at the observed mean of 868.26 mg/L. Extrapolating that published slope linearly, removing 500 mg/L would save about BDT 1,310 per household-year (≈USD 16; my calculation, not the paper's).
- **【Case】** In the same three southwestern districts, a 10-year safe-water project has an NPV of about USD 7.33M and saves BDT 1.70M each year (≈1% of disposable income) using pond sand filtration; Gabura, Munshigonj and three other unions rank highest for migration risk.
