## 標題：鈣鈦礦—矽堆疊太陽能：10 年保固與 25 年融資假設的落差
# Title: Perovskite–Silicon Tandem PV: A 10-Year Module Warranty Against a 25-Year Financing Benchmark
（2026-10-22 21:00 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic

鈣鈦礦—矽堆疊太陽能要在成本上追平矽基，模組究竟要可靠多久？本報告結合同儕審評 LCOE 敏感度、IEA PVPS 劣化機制與 Oxford PV 廠方文件，量化效率與融資年限如何決定它能否提供低成本、低碳電力。

How long must perovskite–silicon tandem modules operate before they beat silicon on cost? Combining a peer-reviewed LCOE sensitivity analysis, IEA PVPS degradation data and Oxford PV warranty documents, this report quantifies how efficiency and financing tenor jointly decide whether tandem delivers low-cost, low-carbon power.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings

- **效率門檻** — Gao 等（2026）以 1,500 全載小時、25 年壽命為矽基基準，LCOE 約 0.157 CNY/kWh；堆疊效率 29% 時須運轉逾 21 年才低於該基準，32% 降至 19 年，35% 需 18 年。堆疊變數 BOS 成本佔比亦由 57% 降至 51%。
- **【機制】** — IEA PVPS T13-30:2025 指出：混鹵相分離（Br 逾 20% 易分離）、可動離子濃度比矽高數個數量級、85°C 光暗循環熱分解、軟弱易損材料，叠加使大面積模組戶外壽命仍不確定，實驗室壽命報告分歧大；效率越高，製程與封裝越難同時穩定。
- **【敏感度】** — 依 Gao 的資本回收公式重算：可融資年限由 25 年縮至 10 年，資本回收項放大 1.74 倍（折現 6%）、1.59 倍（8%）、1.48 倍（10%）；20 年保固於 8% 仍為 1.09 倍。此處只計年限效應，不含殘值、劣化與 O&M。效率升至 32% 時門檻降至 19 年，結論即翻正。
- **保固落差** — Oxford PV 官網稱現行模組「10+ Years lifetime warranty」，Centaur 2LT 資料表列 25% 效率、540 W、10 年壽命。pv Europe 報 Gen 3「Centaur 3」2026 年推出、550 W、26% 效率、15 年保固，2027 年目標 20 年；PES Europe 則稱 15 年屬 Gen 3 原型路線圖，30 年為本十年末目標。
- **現場證據不足** — Brandenburg 廠矽底板名目產能約 100 MW，堆疊線僅「數十 MW」且實際開兩班，部分產能供開發試作，短期難累積 25 年現場劣化樣本。

- **Efficiency threshold** — Gao et al. (2026): 1,500 full-load hours, 25-year life, LCOE ≈ 0.157 CNY/kWh. A 29% tandem needs >21 years to undercut it; 32% → 19 years; 35% → 18 years. Variable BOS 57% → 51%.
- **【機制/Mechanism】** — IEA PVPS T13-30:2025: mixed-halide phase segregation (above ~20% Br), mobile-ion concentrations orders of magnitude above silicon, thermal decomposition over an 85 °C light/dark cycle, mechanically weak films. On large-area modules field lifetime stays unsettled and lab lifetimes dispersed; higher efficiency makes processing and packaging harder to stabilise at once.
- **【敏感度/Sensitivity】** — Gao's capital-recovery term: cutting financeable life 25 → 10 years multiplies it 1.74× at 6% discount, 1.59× at 8%, 1.48× at 10%; a 20-year warranty is still 1.09× at 8%. Tenor only, excluding salvage, degradation, O&M. At 32% efficiency the threshold drops to 19 years and the conclusion reverses.
- **Warranty gap** — Oxford PV's site states "10+ Years lifetime warranty for our module products today"; the Centaur 2LT datasheet lists 25% efficiency, 540 W, 10-year lifetime. pv Europe reports Centaur 3 (2026, 550 W, 26%) with 15 years, targeting 20 in 2027; PES Europe calls 15 years a Gen 3 prototype roadmap figure, 30 a decade-end goal.
- **Thin field evidence** — At Brandenburg, HJT bottom-cell nameplate capacity is ~100 MW; the tandem line is "tens of megawatts", two shifts, part for development campaigns — too little to gather 25-year field degradation data.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact

- **Brandenburg 案例** — 產能結構顯示證據不足有具體成因：底板 100 MW、堆疊數十 MW、兩班運作，商業出貨與研發資源競爭。
- **【推論】融資** — 10 年保固令資本回收項較 25 年基準高 1.59 倍（8% 折現）；若項目仍按 25 年售電模型估值，差額只能由殘值、維修費或產量假設吸收。NREL 估算 25% 效率、3 GW/年美國產能下，2 端子最低可持續價為 0.428 美元/Wdc。
- **【推論】土地與公益** — Gao 估算堆疊變數 BOS 佔比 51%，低於矽基 57%；Oxford PV 目標 2035 年 35% 效率、LCOE 較純矽低約 20%。限地城市與高 BOS 地區或受益最大，前提是壽命風險不轉化為更高貸款利率。
- **安全缺口** — IEA 要求鈣鈦礦吸收層承受約 85°C 完整光暗循環；本題未找到鉛浸出或 RoHS 豁免的可引用量化估計，亦未找到 10 年保固令 25 年項目失去可融資性的公開文件。

- **Brandenburg case** — 100 MW bottom cells, tens of MW tandem, two shifts: commercial output competes with development campaigns — hence thin field data.
- **【推論/Inference】Finance** — A 10-year warranty raises capital recovery 1.59× above a 25-year baseline at 8% discount; pricing a 25-year offtake anyway forces the gap into salvage, maintenance reserve or yield assumptions. NREL puts the minimum sustainable price of a 2-terminal design at US$0.428/Wdc (25% efficiency, 3 GW/yr US output).
- **【推論/Inference】Land and public value** — Variable BOS 51% (tandem) vs 57% (silicon); Oxford PV targets 35% efficiency and ~20% lower LCOE than pure silicon by 2035. Land-constrained cities and high-BOS regions gain most, if lifetime risk does not raise borrowing costs.
- **Safety gap** — IEA requires the absorber to survive an ~85 °C day–night cycle. No citable figure on lead leaching or RoHS exemptions was found, nor any document showing a 10-year warranty making a 25-year project unfinanceable.

### 簡評 / Commentary

- 可信度：模型門檻、IEA 機制、廠方保固三類證據互不依賴；保固數字以廠方文件為準，新聞只作交叉核對。
- 爭議：Gao 門檻依賴 1,500 全載小時與 25 年壽命等中國條件，移植需重算；CRF 倍數只隔離融資年限，非完整 LCOE 差額。
- 缺口：無逐年劣化上限、產品與性能保固分拆，亦無貸款利率或 DSCR 實例。
- 下一步：追蹤 2027 年 20 年保固是否成為合約條款，並累積 25 年以上現場劣化數據。

- Confidence — Three independent evidence chains (LCOE model, IEA mechanisms, manufacturer warranty); warranty figures follow the manufacturer's own documents, press only cross-checks.
- Contested — Gao's threshold assumes 1,500 full-load hours and a 25-year life under Chinese conditions; the CRF multipliers isolate tenor, not the full LCOE gap.
- Gaps — No annual degradation cap, no product/performance warranty split, no lending-rate or DSCR case.
- Next — Track whether the 2027 20-year warranty becomes a contract term; accumulate field degradation data beyond 25 years.
