## 標題：海上風電單樁陰極保護：塗層升級省 79.8% 陽極，能否「順便」降低氫脆風險？
# Title: Offshore Wind Monopile Cathodic Protection: 79.8% Less Anode Mass from Better Coating—Does It Also Reduce Hydrogen Risk?

（2026-10-23 09:44 UTC）

### 主題 / Topic
海上風電單樁靠犧牲陽極陰極保護防腐。問題是：升級塗層省下的鋁陽極，是否等於同時降低氫脆風險。本文以加那利群島一座 8 m 直徑、120 mm 壁厚單樁的 25 年模型為例，拆解電流、塗層劣化與電位三條鏈。它直接關係基礎材料量、海上檢修日數與焊接熱影響區安全。

Offshore wind monopiles rely on sacrificial-anode cathodic protection. This report asks whether a better coating cuts anode mass while also reducing hydrogen-embrittlement risk. A 25-year Canary Islands model—an 8 m diameter, 120 mm wall monopile—is decomposed into current demand, coating breakdown and potential. The result matters for foundation material use, offshore repair days and weld heat-affected-zone safety.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- **【量化】** Taskiran（IJECE, 2022）模型：平均電流需求無塗層 893.69 A、I 級 861.79 A、II 級 364.86 A、III 級 180.95 A。鋁陽極則為 108,732、104,851、44,391、22,016 kg。III 級省 79.8%，I 級只省 3.6%。
- **【機制】** DNV 以 fc=k1+k2·t/2 描述塗層劣化；0–30 m 平均 fc 為 I 級封頂 1、II 級 0.425、III 級 0.2075。四種方案共用同一幾何、水深與水溫，差異只來自 fc，故節省不是環境假設造成。
- **【機制】** 塗層是電流側槓桿，氫是電位側槓桿。DNV-RP-B401（2021）指出，-0.80 至 -1.10 V 區間原子氫產量隨電位更負而指數上升；過保護只指低於 -1.15 V。故多塗一層不等於可採用更負電位。
- **【機制】** DNV 對鐵素體鋼以 SMYS ≤500 MPa 且焊接硬度 ≤350 HV 作 CP 相容門檻；高強度材料則採較嚴電位上限。故氫脆判定必須連接母材、HAZ 硬度與電位紀錄，單憑「有裂縫」不足。
- **【替代解釋】** 海水本身會加速焊接試樣裂紋增速，疲勞與氫可能是並行機制。Havtil 2024 報告調查高強度自升式平台腿與樁靴過渡段氫致裂紋，但其公開頁只列機制範圍，未披露裂縫尺寸或受影響腿數。疲勞與氫的分離仍待材料證據。
- **【敏感度】** 鋁電容量由 2,000 降至 1,500 再至 720 Ah/kg，III 級陽極變 22,016、29,354、61,154 kg；壽命 25→30 年使 fc 由 0.2075 升至 0.245（+18%）；平均電流密度若放大 25%，質量同比例上升。方向不變，只有當 fc ≥1（I 級情況）時「塗層省料」結論失效。

- **【Quantified】** Taskiran's 2022 model gives mean current demand of 893.69 A (uncoated), 861.79 A (Cat I), 364.86 A (Cat II) and 180.95 A (Cat III), with standoff aluminium anode masses of 108,732, 104,851, 44,391 and 22,016 kg. Category III saves 79.8%; Category I saves only 3.6%.
- **【Mechanism】** DNV models coating breakdown as fc=k1+k2·t/2: mean fc at 0–30 m is 1 (capped) for Cat I, 0.425 for Cat II and 0.2075 for Cat III. Geometry, depth and temperature are identical across cases, so only fc drives the saving.
- **【Mechanism】** Coating acts on current; hydrogen acts on potential. DNV-RP-B401 (2021) states atomic-hydrogen production rises exponentially toward the negative end of -0.80 to -1.10 V, and defines over-protection only below -1.15 V. Better coating does not license a more negative potential.
- **【Mechanism】** DNV treats ferritic steel with SMYS ≤500 MPa and weld hardness ≤350 HV as CP-compatible; high-strength grades face tighter potential limits. Hydrogen-cracking attribution must therefore link base metal, HAZ hardness and logged potential.
- **【Alternative explanation】** Seawater itself accelerates crack growth in welded specimens, so fatigue and hydrogen can coexist. Havtil's 2024 report investigates hydrogen-assisted cracking in high-strength jack-up legs and spudcan transitions, but its public page discloses mechanism scope only—no crack size or affected-leg count.
- **【Sensitivity】** At 2,000, 1,500 and 720 Ah/kg, Category III anode mass becomes 22,016, 29,354 and 61,154 kg; extending life from 25 to 30 years raises fc from 0.2075 to 0.245 (+18%); a 25% rise in mean current density scales mass proportionally. Direction is unchanged; the coating saving fails only when fc ≥1, as in Category I.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
- **【推論】** 對 100 座同型單樁，由無塗層改 III 級可少用約 8,672 t 鋁（每樁 86,717 kg）。CP 電流容量隨之下降，意味著陽極、电纜與變壓器選型同步縮小。
- **【推論／可稽核案例】** Dalgic 等（2013）採用主自升式平台每日 150,000 美元日租。一次為期 10 日、單座結構的檢查或維修動員即約 150 萬美元；因此電位規格或 HAZ 硬度出錯，其動員成本可蓋過塗層節省。
- **【推論】** 採購應把「電位窗口＋焊接硬度」列為安全閘，而非只比較塗層級別與陽極重量；後者只能省料，不能為氫脆風險提供豁免。
- **本題未找到可引用的現場氫脆修補成本**：Havtil 公開頁與可取得文獻均未給出裂縫修補金額、船期或工日。

- **【Inference】** For 100 comparable monopiles, moving from uncoated to Category III avoids roughly 8,672 t of aluminium (86,717 kg each). CP current capacity falls with it, shrinking anode, cable and transformer sizing.
- **【Inference; auditable case】** Dalgic et al. (2013) use USD 150,000/day for a main jack-up vessel. A 10-day inspection or repair mobilisation for one structure is therefore about USD 1.5 million, so potential-specification or HAZ-hardness errors can outweigh coating savings through mobilisation alone.
- **【Inference】** Procurement should gate on the potential window and weld-hardness limit, not only coating class and anode mass. The latter saves material; it does not grant a hydrogen-embrittlement exemption.
- No citable field repair cost for hydrogen cracking was found: neither the Havtil public page nor accessible literature discloses crack-repair cost, vessel days or labour days.
