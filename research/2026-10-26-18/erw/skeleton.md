### 1.4 UNDERREPORTING — the key uncertainty (LM2025, p.98-99, "Casualties in States Parties in 2024")
- "Many mine/ERW casualties, however, **go unrecorded each year globally**, including in States Parties, and
  therefore are not captured in sources that contribute to the combined totals."
- "Some countries, especially those experiencing conflict, **do not have functional casualty surveillance
  systems** in place." Other reporting "often inadequate in some way, or lack disaggregation."
- "in some contexts, the number of recorded mine/ERW casualties could appear **significantly lower than what
  would be expected given the recognized scale of contamination**."
- Cross-check (Ukraine): Monitor recorded **293** casualties in 2024; **OHCHR recorded 283** (74 killed,
  209 injured) for CY2024; OHCHR total **1,379 (413 killed, 966 injured)** mine/ERW casualties from the
  Russian invasion (2022) to end-2024.
- Cross-check (Yemen): Monitor **265**; CIMP 2024 Annual Report found **260 civilian casualties** from mines and ERW.
- Conclusion drawn by the source: the congruence "is indicative of a wider and persistent problem of limited data
  availability, unreliable surveillance systems, and inadequate reporting mechanisms, meaning that casualties in
  these countries are **consistently under-reported each year across multiple recording mechanisms**."
  => The 6,279 figure is a FLOOR, not an estimate of total.

### 1.5 Funding for mine action 2024 (LM2025, "FUNDING FOR MINE ACTION")
- Global funding **US$1.07 billion** in 2024 (2nd year >$1bn; +4% vs $1.03bn in 2023).
- International funding **$761 million** (-5% vs 2023). National contributions **$306.3 million** from 26 affected
  states (>30% of global funding).
- Clearance (incl. integrated programs) = **75% ($571.3 million)** of international funding.
- Capacity building $66.3m (9%); **$32.8m of that was EU funding for capacity-building in Ukraine**.
- Victim assistance = **$36.4 million (5% of total)**, a 23% decrease from 2023. 66% went to 4 states
  (Afghanistan, Syria, Ukraine, Yemen).
- 47 states + 2 other areas received $689.9m from 40 donors. Top 10 recipients got $550.3m (72%).
  **Ukraine = top recipient 3 years running: $252.4 million (33% of all international funds)**, then Iraq $60.8m,
  Yemen $47.1m.
- 67% of international funding via international NGOs; only **2% to national NGOs**.
- Funding for national mine action programs is "often under-reported" (LM2025 p.~ "Few States Parties report...").

AOAV Explosive Violence Monitor 2024: **67,026 deaths and injuries** from explosive weapons, **59,524 civilians (89%)** —
highest since 2010. URL: https://aoav.org.uk/2025/explosive-violence-monitor-2024/
AOAV Explosive Violence Monitor 2025: **49,814 deaths and injuries**, 45,362 civilians (91%). URL: https://aoav.org.uk/2026/explosive-violence-monitor-2025/

*** IMPORTANT DISTINCTION FOR THE REPORT ***
Explosive-weapons deaths (28,600 in 2024) >> ERW casualty figure (6,279 in 2024). These are DIFFERENT populations:
- explosive weapons deaths = deaths caused by the USE of explosive weapons during conflict (a conflict-intensity metric);
- ERW casualties = injuries/deaths caused by RESIDUAL explosive ordnance (mines, UXO) after/away from active bombardment.
The ratio (6,279 / 28,600 ≈ 22%) is a rough indicator of how much the "conflict" burden dwarfs the "residue" burden —
but they are not nested sets and must not be added.

### 1.4b Underreporting quantification (indirect but real)
- Gaza case (EWM 2024, Box 2): LSHTM/Lancet capture-recapture analysis estimated direct deaths from traumatic injury
  in Gaza 7 Oct 2023–30 Jun 2024 were **more than 64,000, ~40% higher than the >41,900 reported by Gaza MoH**.
- EWM 2024 methodology note: "these challenges almost certainly result in **under-reporting of civilian fatalities
  across the globe**". AOAV estimated English-language media captured only ~1/3 of civilian deaths in Gaza in Oct 2023;
  ACLED estimated only **half** of reported fatalities were captured in relevant events.
- Indirect deaths: Lancet analysis of recent conflicts estimates indirect deaths range from **3 to 15 times** direct deaths;
  applied to Gaza this implies up to **186,000+ additional indirect deaths** (~8% of Gaza population).
  URL: https://www.hi-us.org/sn_uploads/document/EWM_Annual-Report_2024.pdf
  (This is the strongest published evidence that the ERW/UXO casualty floor is a floor.)
