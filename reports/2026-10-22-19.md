# 標題：半熟料水泥為何仍有同等強度：煅燒黏土與石灰石的協同機制
# Title: Why half-clinker cement keeps its strength: the synergy of calcined clay and limestone

### 主題 / Topic
本報告回答一個材料科學問題：熟料減半後，水泥為何仍有普通矽酸鹽水泥的強度？研究指出偏高嶺土與石灰石重新分配了鋁酸鹽功能，而非單靠填充效應。本文檢視機制鏈、量化減碳，並核對工業裝置與規範。

This report asks a materials-science question: when clinker is halved, why can cement still match ordinary Portland cement in strength? The answer is that metakaolin and limestone redistribute the cement's aluminate function, rather than merely acting as filler. It examines that causal chain, quantifies the decarbonisation, and checks industrial plants and standards.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- **材料組成**：LC3-50 含 50% 熟料、30% 煅燒黏土、15% 石灰石、5% 石膏；黏土須含至少 40% 高嶺土。
- **【機制】反應鏈**：偏高嶺土與石灰石生成碳鋁酸鹽，補償熟料減少後的鋁酸鹽功能。石灰石溶出碳酸根，C-S-H 吸附後釋放氫氧根，推動水化產物生長。
- **【機制】排除替代解釋**：石英填充不形成碳鋁酸鹽，故單靠顆粒填充不能解釋結果。孔隙細化即使令早期水化放慢，碳鋁酸鹽反應仍支持後期強度上升。
- **減碳量化**：IEA 2DS 把熟料因子由 0.65 降至 0.60，累計省 29 億噸二氧化碳。2025 年全球水泥產量約 38 億噸，每降 1 個百分點少用 3,800 萬噸熟料。
- **工業與規範**：法國 Saint-Pierre-la-Cour 年產最多 50 萬噸低碳水泥，稱較 CEM I 減碳 50%。迦納 CBI 全球最大閃速爐年產逾 40 萬噸煅燒黏土。EN 197-5、印度及美國規範已允許此類配方。

- **Composition**: LC3-50 is 50% clinker, 30% calcined clay, 15% limestone and 5% gypsum; the clay needs at least 40% kaolinite.
- **【Mechanism】 reaction chain**: metakaolin and limestone form carboaluminates, compensating for the aluminate function lost with clinker. Limestone releases carbonate that C-S-H sorbs, then C-S-H releases hydroxide, driving hydrate growth.
- **【Mechanism】 alternatives ruled out**: quartz forms no carboaluminate, so packing alone cannot explain the result. Even when pore refinement slows early hydration, the carboaluminate reaction sustains later strength gain.
- **Quantified decarbonisation**: the IEA 2DS pathway lowers the clinker factor from 0.65 to 0.60, cumulatively saving 2.9 Gt CO2. With about 3.8 Gt of cement produced in 2025, each percentage point cuts about 38 Mt of clinker demand.
- **Plants and standards**: Saint-Pierre-la-Cour in France makes up to 500,000 t/yr of low-carbon cement, reported 50% below CEM I. CBI in Ghana runs the world's largest flash calciner at over 400,000 t/yr. EN 197-5, Indian and US standards now allow such formulations.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
- **已核實規模**：迦納裝置年產逾 40 萬噸煅燒黏土；法國產線年產最多 50 萬噸低碳水泥，稱較 CEM I 減碳 50%。
- **【推論】**：以約 0.51 噸二氧化碳每噸熟料計，若迦納 40 萬噸煅燒黏土等量替代熟料，年可少排約 20 萬噸二氧化碳。實際值取決於未披露的替代率。
- **【推論】**：聯合國工業化組織估計每噸水泥可省燃料約 7.7 美元，碳價生效另加約 25 歐元。百萬噸級產線每年燃料帳單約減 770 萬美元。

- **Verified scale**: the Ghana installation makes over 400,000 t/yr of calcined clay; the French line makes up to 500,000 t/yr of low-carbon cement, reported 50% below CEM I.
- **【Inference】**: at about 0.51 t CO2 per tonne of clinker, if Ghana's 400,000 t of calcined clay displaced an equal mass of clinker, roughly 200,000 t CO2 per year would be avoided. The real figure depends on an undisclosed substitution ratio.
- **【Inference】**: UNIDO estimates fuel savings near USD 7.7 per tonne of cement, plus about EUR 25 where carbon is priced. A one-million-tonne plant would cut its annual fuel bill by roughly USD 7.7 million.

### 簡評 / Commentary
**可信度**：機制鏈有同行評審回顧與工業試驗支持，可信度較高；實廠節碳幅度可信度中等，因法國與迦納均未披露實際熟料替代率。缺口包括長期碳化與氯鹽侵入、同場普通水泥對照數據、各廠絕對減碳量，以及原料黏土與石灰石的適用門檻。

**Confidence**: the mechanism is supported by peer-reviewed reviews and industrial trials, so confidence is relatively high; confidence in plant-level savings is medium because neither France nor Ghana discloses the actual clinker substitution ratio. Gaps remain on long-term carbonation and chloride ingress, paired ordinary-cement controls, absolute avoided CO2 per plant, and feedstock quality thresholds.
