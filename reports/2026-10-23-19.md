### 主題 / Topic
美軍兒童血鉛檢測率下降，是否足以證明20.3萬套私營家庭住宅已無鉛害？本報告比較全軍檢驗與聯邦審計，區分真實改善、檢測偏差及房屋暴露缺口。核心問題是：下降曲線能否代表暴露源已受控？
Does a falling blood-lead testing rate among military children prove that the roughly 203,000 privately operated family housing units are lead-free? This report compares service-wide screening with federal audit findings to separate genuine improvement, sampling bias, and housing exposure gaps.

### 核心發現 / Key Findings
- **【機制】** 1980年前住宅的含鉛漆可剝落、磨損成塵；兒童手口攝入後吸收，抬升血鉛並傷害神經發育。軍方兒童與住宅年齡的交叉資料仍缺。
  Lead-based paint in pre-1980 housing can flake and abrade into dust, which children ingest by hand-to-mouth behaviour, elevating blood lead and impairing neurodevelopment. Service-wide child data are still not cross-tabulated against housing age.
- **【量化】** 2010至2017年，軍方為162,238名兒童進行177,061次檢測；保留169,917次年度最高值後，1,334次異常，比例為0.79%。各軍種均呈下降趨勢。
  From 2010 to 2017 the military performed 177,061 tests on 162,238 children; after keeping one annual maximum per child (169,917 retained values), 1,334 were abnormal — 0.79%. Every branch shows a declining trend.
- **【機制】** 2021年八個月，國防部報告30,412名兒童受篩查、12,044名接受檢測、83名血鉛異常，即受檢者0.69%。但部分醫療設施資料不全，審計認為很可能低估。
  In the first eight months of 2021, DoD reported 30,412 children screened, 12,044 tested, and 83 with abnormal blood lead — 0.69% of those tested. Because some facilities had incomplete records, the audit judged the true figure likely higher.
- **【排除的替代解釋】** 標準化監測令下降趨勢較可信，但受檢兒童仍屬被選樣本。未測兒童、住房年齡及維修紀錄未交叉，不能排除檢測選擇造成部分下降。
  Standardised monitoring makes the decline more credible, but tested children are still a selected sample. Untested children, housing age, and remediation records are not cross-tabulated, so sampling selection cannot be ruled out as a contributor to the decline.
- **【敏感度】** 若0.79%適用於一萬名受檢兒童，約有79名異常；減半為40名、倍增為158名。只有分母與選樣相同，這個數字才可解讀為群體比率。
  If the 0.79% rate applies to 10,000 tested children, roughly 79 would be abnormal; halving gives 40 and doubling gives 158. The figure reads as a population rate only if the denominator and selection are held constant.

### 潛在影響 / Potential Impact
- **【推論】** 截至2022財年，78份協議、14家公司營運約203,000套住宅，涵蓋99%美國境內軍隊家庭住宅。兒童檢測、房齡及含鉛漆維修若不連通，下降趨勢仍可能漏掉局部暴露熱點。
  As of FY2022, 78 agreements with 14 companies operated roughly 203,000 housing units, covering 99% of military family housing in the United States. Unless child testing, housing age, and lead-paint remediation are linked, the declining trend can still miss localised exposure hotspots.
- **【具體案例】** 2021年八個月聯邦審計個案確認83名異常兒童，且確認資料存在缺口；這比全美人口比率更接近可稽核的軍方健康結果。
  The FY2021 eight-month federal audit confirmed 83 children with abnormal results and confirmed that records have gaps; this is closer to an auditable military health outcome than a national population rate.
- **【成本敏感度】** 以1999年HUD非軍方單價作錨，1,000套全整治約900萬美元，10,000套約9,000萬美元；臨時控制則為250萬及2,500萬美元。未取得軍方房齡與危害比例，不能外推至全部203,000套。
  Using 1999 HUD non-military unit prices as the anchor, full remediation of 1,000 units costs about USD 9 million and 10,000 units about USD 90 million; interim controls cost USD 2.5 million and USD 25 million respectively. Without military housing-age and hazard-proportion data, this cannot be extrapolated to all 203,000 units.
- 本題未找到可引用的軍方兒童暴露總人數或軍宅含鉛漆比例。優先工作應是連結住房年代、檢測結果及整改完成率，而非只追蹤血鉛門檻。
  No citable total for military child exposure counts or the share of military housing with lead paint was found. The priority work is to link housing vintage, test results, and remediation completion rates rather than tracking blood-lead thresholds alone.

### 簡評 / Commentary
可信度：檢驗數量及受測兒童異常率為中高；由檢測下降推論軍宅已安全則可信度偏低。不同年代判定門檻、資料缺漏及受檢者選樣均限制跨期比較。
Confidence: moderate-to-high on test volumes and the abnormal rate among tested children; low on any inference that military housing is now lead-free. Differing thresholds across eras, missing records, and selection into testing all limit cross-period comparison.
爭議點：檢測率下降至少有三種解釋——真實暴露改善、檢測覆蓋變窄、以及判定門檻或數據管線變化。本報告只能區分「哪些解釋未被排除」，不能定奪主因。
Contested point: a falling testing rate has at least three candidate explanations — genuine exposure reduction, narrowing test coverage, and changes in thresholds or data pipelines. This report can say which explanations remain open, not which dominates.
資料缺口：軍方兒童與住宅年齡/維修紀錄的交叉資料、含鉛漆占比、軍方實際整治單價，全部未取得。1999年HUD單價只可作量級錨，不可作預算依據。
Data gaps: no cross-tabulation of military children against housing age and remediation records, no lead-paint prevalence in military housing, and no military-specific remediation pricing. The 1999 HUD figures serve only as an order-of-magnitude anchor, not a budgeting basis.
